Remarks

Both anchors are fractions: zero is the leading edge, 0.5 is the middle, and one is the trailing edge. new ScrollAnchor(0, 1) means the element's top reaches the viewport bottom, while new ScrollAnchor(1, 0) means its bottom reaches the viewport top. Those anchors define the default progress sweep from zero to one.
