Class RainGlass

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Rain on a misted pane over what is under it: beads that come and go, fine droplets, and drops that slide down in fits and starts, leaving a clear wet trail dotted with droplets. Each drop is a small lens showing what is behind it upside down. Fog mists the glass between them. Under reduced motion the drops hold still.
