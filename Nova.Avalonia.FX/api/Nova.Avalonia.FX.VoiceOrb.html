Class VoiceOrb

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

A living orb of color for voice input: three soft lights drift inside a glassy sphere, their colors meeting smoothly, with a glow around it. Bind Level to the microphone level: the orb swells, its edge rolls and the lights drift faster as it rises. Level changes are eased (quick to rise, slower to fall), so a raw meter reading looks smooth.
