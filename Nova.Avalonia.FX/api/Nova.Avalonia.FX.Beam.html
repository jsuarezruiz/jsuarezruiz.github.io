Light traveling along a curve between two points, over a faint track: data flowing from one control to another in a diagram, an integration page or a pipeline. The ends are From and To, shares of the host's shape, or the centers of FromElement and ToElement, which it follows as the layout changes; Curvature bows the path. Put the host around the panel that holds both controls, so the beam runs under them. Under reduced motion it holds a still gradient.
