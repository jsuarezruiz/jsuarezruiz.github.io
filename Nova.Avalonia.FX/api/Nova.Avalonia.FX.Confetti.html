Class Confetti

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX

A burst of confetti that flies, tumbles, flutters and falls: for a success or a milestone. Every piece is a pure function of the time since its burst, and bursts overlap. Under reduced motion the pieces appear in place and fade without flying.
