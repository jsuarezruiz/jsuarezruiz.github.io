Class Fog

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Banks of mist drifting across the shape at DriftSpeed, thickest at the bottom and thinning out toward Height. Over the content it hangs in front of it; under a see-through panel it is a background. Under reduced motion it holds still.
