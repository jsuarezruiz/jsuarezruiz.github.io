Class Ascii

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Redraws what is behind it (the host's content, and anything under the host) as text: each cell shows the character whose ink matches the brightness there, from " .-:+=%*#@". The glyphs are small dot shapes made in the shader, not a font. With IsColored, each character takes the content's own color. Fade it in and out with Intensity.
