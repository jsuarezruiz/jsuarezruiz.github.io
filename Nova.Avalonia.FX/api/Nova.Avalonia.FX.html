Namespace Nova.Avalonia.FX

Classes

AmbientGlow

A glow in the content's own colors: the child (album art, a video frame, a photo, an illustration) blurred, spread out and lit behind itself, like a screen's ambient light on the wall. The host captures the child's pixels when it changes; call RefreshMask() after changing something deep inside it, or every frame for a video.

Ascii

Redraws what is behind it (the host's content, and anything under the host) as text: each cell shows the character whose ink matches the brightness there, from " .-:+=%*#@". The glyphs are small dot shapes made in the shader, not a font. With IsColored, each character takes the content's own color. Fade it in and out with Intensity.

Aurora

Three light curtains drifting across the shape, each with a bright lower hem and noise rays, feathered inside the shape's edge. Under reduced motion they hold one pose.

Beam

Light traveling along a curve between two points, over a faint track: data flowing from one control to another in a diagram, an integration page or a pipeline. The ends are From and To, shares of the host's shape, or the centers of FromElement and ToElement, which it follows as the layout changes; Curvature bows the path. Put the host around the panel that holds both controls, so the beam runs under them. Under reduced motion it holds a still gradient.

Beams

A background of shafts of light that drift slowly sideways, with light falling down them: a quiet, cinematic backdrop for a hero section or a dark landing page. The shafts can lean with Angle; as a fill it paints the shape under the child.

Bokeh

Soft out-of-focus lights drifting at three depths over a background, as a lens renders lights it isn't focused on: discs with brighter rims, bigger and faster in front. A background fill, drawn under the child; to lay the lights over the content, set BackgroundColor's alpha to 0 and Placement to Over.

BorderBeam

Streaks of light running around the border, fading from a bright head to the tail color, with a glow either side: for a "new" badge, a chat input or a card that is working. Under reduced motion the streaks hold still.

Caustics

Caustics: the shifting web of light that water throws on the floor of a pool, over a water color. With a transparent WaterColor, Placement Over and BlendMode Screen it lays pool light over the host's content instead.

ChromaSplit

Red and blue pulled apart from green over what is behind (and the host's child), with optional glitch: now and then a thin slice jumps sideways.

CodeRain

Columns of glyphs raining down a terminal-style background: each stream has a bright head and a fading trail, and the glyphs flip as it passes. The glyphs are dot-matrix shapes made up per cell (no font involved), so it looks the same everywhere. A background fill, drawn under the child; to rain over the content, set BackgroundColor's alpha to 0 and Placement to Over.

ColorFilter

CSS-style color filters, live, within the shape: over the child (the default) they recolor the child and whatever shows through it, as CSS filter does; under it (Placement Under) they recolor what is behind the host, as backdrop-filter does. With UseMask only the child's own pixels change (an icon, not the card around it). Brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, grayscale and sepia use CSS's formulas; Intensity is how much of the filter shows. Disabled states, "grayscale until hover", styled photos, images dimmed in dark mode. It starts as grayscale (Preset).

Confetti

A burst of confetti that flies, tumbles, flutters and falls: for a success or a milestone. Every piece is a pure function of the time since its burst, and bursts overlap. Under reduced motion the pieces appear in place and fade without flying.

Crt

An old CRT over what is behind (and the host's child): barrel bulge, scanlines, a color split, an aperture grille, a soft glow, a rolling bar and a vignette.

Dither

Ordered dithering of a tone into a palette (dark to light), in square pixels anchored to the shape. Two colors is one-bit; more is a palette.

DotGrid

A grid of dots or squares that flicker at random: a quiet, technical background. Dots near the pointer light up and grow, and a press (or Ripple(Point?)) sends a ring out across the grid.

Electric

Lightning: a fractal bolt that strikes with a flash, holds, sometimes return-strokes, and re-strikes, mostly down the same channel. The previous strikes linger as afterimages, and forks shoot out at each strike, cooling from white through the tint to violet. Zap(Point?) fires a single strike.

FXColorList

An ordered list of colors. In XAML: Colors="#2b1b5a, #ff6f91, #ffc46b".

FXColorListConverter
FXDiagnostics

Opt-in timing of layer draws, for benchmarks and profiling. Off by default, and free while off. With MeasureGpu on, every draw also waits for the GPU to finish it, which slows frames down: use it to compare layers, not in a shipping app.

FXFill

A fill paints the shape (or, with BorderWidth, a band inside its edge) and goes under the child as its background. Under reduced motion every fill holds one fixed pose.

FXHost

Hosts one child and draws FX layers around it: under the child (glows, pulses, fills) and over it (sheens, grain, ripples). The layers follow the child's shape: its bounds and corner radius, taken from CornerRadius or, when that is unset, from the child itself. Layers render on the compositor thread and only tick while something moves, and while the host can be seen: a hidden host, or one scrolled out of view, asks for no frames.

Light that spills past the child (a glow's reach) is drawn outside the host's bounds, so it is cut by any clipping ancestor: a ScrollViewer, a Border with ClipToBounds, and Fluent's ContentControl, which clips by default. Leave room around the host inside such containers, or set ClipToBounds="False" where that is safe.

FXLayer

Base class of every layer. A layer is a pure function of (position, parameters, time, seed): no hidden state and no randomness, so a given time always renders the same frame. Layers are styled elements: they take part in styles, bindings and transitions, so a style setter on Intensity with a DoubleTransition animates a glow smoothly on hover. A layer is in one FXHost at a time: adding it to a second host's Layers throws until it is removed from the first.

FXLayerCollection

The layers of an FXHost.

FXPaletteFill

A fill painted from a list of colors (up to six): mesh gradient, swirl, waves, grain gradient, voronoi, metaballs, dither and bokeh. Unset Colors uses the preset's palette.

FXSettings

Global switches shared by every layer.

FXTransitionLayer

A layer that shows and hides its host's content with a transition (Reveal, Genie, Shatter). Set IsShown, or call Show(Point?) or Hide(Point?): the layer captures the content as it looks, hides the real element and plays the transition on the capture, then hands back to the element once shown. While hidden the element is invisible but keeps its place in the layout. With nothing to capture (no content, invisible, or not laid out yet), or while inactive, it switches at once. Duration sets the pace; the layer's Speed doesn't apply.

Fire

Fire off the top edge: domain-warped turbulence whose warp grows with height, so tongues curl, narrow and tear; a flicker per column; a temperature ramp from a white-yellow base to red tips and a faint veil of smoke. Embers are flung up, slow under drag to a buoyant drift, sway and cool. Plasma is a thin breathing band of filaments around the border.

Simulated and Smoke run a small fluid simulation instead: heat rises, curls, drifts with Wind, cools and turns to smoke. It steps a small grid on the render thread every frame (well under a millisecond); Intensity is how much heat is fed in.

Fireworks

Fireworks over the host: a rocket climbs from the bottom edge and bursts into a sphere of streaking sparks that slow, fall, crackle and fade. Fire(Point?) sends one to a point (or a spot of its own), FireOnPress fires where you press, and Interval keeps a show going. Shots overlap. Light adds on dark surfaces, color shows on light ones.

FlutedGlass

Reeded glass over what is behind the host: parallel convex ribs, each a cylindrical lens that shows a squeezed, flipped strip of the backdrop, with a color split toward its edges, a sheen and a shaded groove.

Fog

Banks of mist drifting across the shape at DriftSpeed, thickest at the bottom and thinning out toward Height. Over the content it hangs in front of it; under a see-through panel it is a background. Under reduced motion it holds still.

Frost

Ice that creeps in from the shape's edges in feathery crystal branches, frosting what is under it (the content, for an Over layer): blurred, whitened toward Tint, with glints. Amount is how far it has crept in; animate it with a transition to freeze or thaw. It melts in a ragged circle around the pointer and freezes back when the pointer leaves.

Genie

Hides the content by pulling it into a point, like a window minimizing into the dock, and pours it back out to show it. The sides bend in toward the point first, then the content slides down the funnel into it. The point is the center of Target (a dock icon, a button), the point given to Hide(Point?) or Show(Point?), or the bottom center of the content. The content is captured and hidden while it plays, and handed back once shown. Under reduced motion it fades.

The funnel draws outside the host on its way to the point, so it is cut by any clipping ancestor between the content and the target.

Glass

A pane of liquid glass over what is behind the host: a beveled rim that refracts and splits the backdrop, a little magnification, an optional frost, a specular rim facing the light and a soft drop shadow. It reads what is drawn behind it, so it follows anything that moves there.

Glitch

A digital glitch over the content: bands of it tear sideways, blocks jump, the color channels split, a few rows flash in the glitch colors and scanlines show. It changes in steps (about 20 a second), as a broken signal does. It draws nothing between glitches, so a burst layer costs nothing until it fires: a short burst on Trigger() or a press (an error, a denied action), or, with IsContinuous, now and then on its own. Under reduced motion a burst is a short, mild color split and a continuous glitch stays still.

Glow

Bloom around the shape. The light is a tight Gaussian plus a wide one through a soft knee, so a tinted glow keeps its hue instead of clipping to white. Switching IsLit off cools the light (it shrinks, deepens and dims) instead of cutting it.

Grain

Film grain inside the shape, per device pixel. Static by default, so it never shimmers.

GrainGradient

A linear, radial or conic gradient whose colors scatter into film grain: triangular noise scatters the ramp position, so colors stipple into each other, plus brightness grain.

Halftone

Print-style round dots on a rotated screen, sized by a tone; neighboring dots merge where the tone is dark.

HeatHaze

Shimmering hot air: what is behind wobbles in rising, turbulent noise. By default it rises above the shape, strongest a little up the column (pair it with Fire); with WholeShape it fills the shape.

HoloFoil

Holographic foil over the shape: an iridescent rainbow sheen whose bands shift as the pointer moves over it, fine etched lines, glitter that flares as the angle passes each facet, and a soft glare under the pointer. Still when the pointer is away (unless it drifts), so idle it costs nothing.

Ink

Colored ink in a fluid over the content: moving the pointer pushes the fluid and leaves ink in a color that changes as it goes, a press throws a splash of every color, and the ink swirls, spreads and fades. A small fluid simulation runs on the render thread only while there is ink; with none it draws nothing and costs nothing. Under reduced motion the ink stays where it lands and fades.

InnerShadow

Light and shade inside the child's own shape, its text or icons: a pressed-in (inset), raised (emboss) or letterpress look, lit from LightAngle. It follows the shape exactly (from the distance to its edge, which the host measures when it captures the child), so it works on glyphs, not just boxes. After changing something deep inside the child, call RefreshMask().

Jelly

Pressed, the element squashes and wobbles like jelly before it settles: a damped spring that squashes it down and out, then overshoots, plus a bulge that spreads from the press point. While it wobbles the layer draws a capture of the content (the real content is hidden and still takes input), and hands back to it once settled. Under reduced motion the wobble is a small, quick squash.

LightLeaks

Warm film light leaking in over the shape's edges: four long, soft glows that drift along the frame and breathe in and out, whitening where they are hottest. Added as light over the content (a photo, a hero card); over light content it draws as tinted haze.

LightRays

Shafts of light falling across the shape from a source just outside it, like sun through a window: a fan of rays that shimmer and shift slowly, bright where they come in and fading with distance. Added light, as tinted haze on a light surface. Under reduced motion the rays hold still.

LiquidMetal

A polished metal slab reflecting a photo studio: a rolled rim, a domed face and a flowing, domain-warped surface, with conductor Fresnel and optional thin-film color. As a border it is a round tube.

LiquidProgress

A liquid filling the shape to Level: for progress bars, gauges and battery or storage levels. The surface waves, bubbles rise, and a change of level eases in with a little overshoot and sloshes the surface. Bind Level to a progress value (0..1).

Magnifier

A glass loupe that follows the pointer over the host and magnifies what is under it: the content and what is behind it. It grows in when the pointer arrives and fades when it leaves; PointAt(Point?) aims it from code (a tour, keyboard focus).

MeshGradient

Up to six colors pinned to points that drift slowly, blended smoothly in OKLab (so blends never go muddy) with Shepard weights: each color owns its point, blends fall off smoothly.

Metaballs

Glossy blobs that drift on slow paths and merge, over a base color. Each blob takes one of the colors in turn.

MotionBlur

A smear of the content along its motion, behind it, while the host moves: a slide-in, a drag, a fling, a transform animation. It reads how the host moves in its window every frame, so any movement counts (scrolling too). Still, it draws nothing. The host captures the content when it changes; call RefreshMask() after changing something deep inside it.

OldFilm

What is behind it as an old print running through a projector: toned toward sepia, darker at the corners and grained, weaving a little in the gate and flickering at 18 frames a second, with scratches that run down it for a few frames and specks of dust that come and go. Under reduced motion it holds one frame.

Outline

A stroke around the child's own shape, its text, icons or a cut-out picture, at an exact width whatever the shape (from the distance to its edge, which the host measures when it captures the child). Sticker adds a soft shadow under a thick border. Outside strokes go under the child, centered ones over it. After changing something deep inside the child, call RefreshMask().

PagePeel

Hovered, a corner of the element curls up like a sticker or a page and shows its back, with a shadow under the curl; it peels further as the pointer nears the corner and settles back when the pointer leaves. IsPeeled holds it peeled from code. While peeled the layer draws a capture of the content (the real content is hidden and still takes input).

ParticleNetwork

Dots drifting across the shape, joined by fine lines whenever two come close: the closer, the stronger the line. The pointer joins in as one more point that draws the nearby dots toward it. Drawn with Skia directly; each dot's path is a function of time, so a given time always draws the same frame. Under reduced motion the dots hold still.

Plastic

A plastic slab under one soft light: a diffuse base that keeps its color, a GGX highlight widened by the light's size, and a faint sky at grazing angles. Still, so it costs nothing per frame once drawn.

PointerShadow

The pointer is a light over the page and the content casts a soft shadow away from it, in the shape of the content's own pixels: further and softer the further the light is, up to MaxOffset. Within PointerReach of the content the light follows the pointer across the whole window, so every card on a page casts its shadow away from it; beyond that, or with no pointer, the light rests above the content and the shadow falls just below it.

ProgressiveBlur

What is behind the shape goes from sharp to fully blurred toward one Edge: a header or a tab bar over a scrolling list, where content fades into the bar instead of meeting a hard line. The ramp crossfades five copies of the backdrop (sharp, then blurred by an eighth, a quarter, a half and all of Blur), so every point sees a real blur, not a mix of sharp and blurred. Tint fades toward the bar's own color.

Pulse

Rings and halos that follow the shape's outline, tap ripples, and pings that fly to a destination.

RainGlass

Rain on a misted pane over what is under it: beads that come and go, fine droplets, and drops that slide down in fits and starts, leaving a clear wet trail dotted with droplets. Each drop is a small lens showing what is behind it upside down. Fog mists the glass between them. Under reduced motion the drops hold still.

RetroGrid

A synthwave floor: glowing grid lines running toward a horizon under a gradient sky, with a striped sun. For hero sections and splash screens; as a fill it paints the shape under the child.

Reveal

Shows or hides the host's content with a dissolve, a burn, a pixelate, an iris, a wipe, a melt, a clock wipe, a grid of diamonds, a swirl, halftone dots, or noise resolving into it. Set IsShown (or call Show(Point?) and Hide(Point?)): the layer captures the content as it looks, hides the real element and plays the transition on the capture, then hands back to the element. While hidden the element is invisible but keeps its place in the layout. With nothing to capture yet (invisible, or not laid out) it switches at once. Under reduced motion it fades.

Shatter

Breaks the content into glass shards that fly apart and fall, or turns it to dust that blows away (hide), and brings it back together (show): for delete, dismiss and undo. Set IsShown, or call Hide(Point?) and Show(Point?) with the point the break starts from. Shards are cut around that point, smaller near it; dust leaves from that point outward or, with no point, from the side the wind blows toward. The pieces are drawn with Skia directly from a capture of the content, which is hidden while they fly. Under reduced motion it is a short fade.

Shimmer

A sheen band sweeping across the shape: skeleton loaders and "Thinking…" surfaces. The band starts and ends off the shape, so it never cuts. Under reduced motion the surface breathes.

Shockwave

A one-shot ring of compressed air that bends what is behind it as it spreads from a point, with a color split at the front, a highlight where there is content to catch it, and a faint rim so it still reads over a plain backdrop. Shots overlap.

Silk

A background of soft satin folds that flow slowly: a few warped folds lit by one soft light, shading into their creases with a broad sheen along the ridges. For hero sections, sign-in pages and cards; as a fill it paints the shape under the child.

Sparkles

Crisp four-point stars around the shape, each with its own size (mostly small, a few large), twinkle rate and spin. Continuous stars rest between appearances so only a few are lit at once. Burst() pops every star once and flings it out a little.

Spoiler

Hides the host's content until it is revealed, as messaging apps hide spoilers: shimmering dust over a heavy blur, a mosaic, or a solid cover. A tap opens it in a soft circle from the tap point; IsRevealed binds both ways, and HideAfter covers it again. Wrap a TextBlock (or any element) in an FXHost with this layer.

Spotlight

A soft light that follows the pointer across the shape and lights the border nearest to it, fading in when the pointer arrives and out when it leaves. It also reacts to a pointer just outside the shape (within PointerReach), so the border lights up as the pointer approaches. Idle, it draws nothing and costs nothing.

Starfield

A night sky: twinkling stars in three depths, shooting stars, or warp streaks.

Swirl

Colors stirred into a slow swirl: a twist around the center over twice domain-warped noise, through the color ramp (blended in OKLab).

Tilt

The host leans toward the pointer in 3D and grows a little, and a glare slides across it with the pointer, like a card turned under a light. The lean is a perspective render transform on the host, so the host's other layers (a glow, a shadow) lean with it; it eases on every frame while it moves and is removed once the host settles flat. While it leans it takes the place of a render transform set on the host itself. Under reduced motion the host stays flat and only the glare follows the pointer.

Topography

The contour lines of a slowly changing landscape, like a topographic map: thin lines of even width wherever the slope is, every fifth one bolder. A calm background for a hero or an empty state. Under reduced motion it holds still.

Trail

A trail behind the pointer as it moves over the host: a glowing ribbon or a stream of sparks, head color to tail color. A still pointer leaves nothing, so idle it costs nothing; under reduced motion there is no trail.

VoiceOrb

A living orb of color for voice input: three soft lights drift inside a glassy sphere, their colors meeting smoothly, with a glow around it. Bind Level to the microphone level: the orb swells, its edge rolls and the lights drift faster as it rises. Level changes are eased (quick to rise, slower to fall), so a raw meter reading looks smooth.

VoiceWave

Glowing waves across the shape that swell with the voice: three colors crossing each other under an envelope that pinches them to nothing at the ends, lying almost flat and drifting slowly when quiet. Bind Level to the microphone level; changes are eased (quick to rise, slower to fall), and the waves drift faster as it rises, without jumping. Pairs with VoiceOrb.

Voronoi

Cells around seed points that orbit slowly, with exact seams and cells shaded toward their rim. Each cell takes one of the colors.

Water

The content under a sheet of water: moving the pointer over it drags ripples across the surface, a press drops a splash, and Rain lets drops fall on their own. The waves bend what is under them and catch the light. A small wave simulation runs on the render thread only while the water moves; calm water draws nothing and costs nothing.

Waves

Layered bands of color, one per color from back to front, with rolling, swaying crests; each front band shades the one behind it.

Weather

Rain streaks or snowflakes falling in three depths, slanted by the wind, over the shape.
