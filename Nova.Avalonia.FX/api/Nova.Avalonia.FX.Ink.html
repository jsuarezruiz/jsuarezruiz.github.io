Class Ink

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Colored ink in a fluid over the content: moving the pointer pushes the fluid and leaves ink in a color that changes as it goes, a press throws a splash of every color, and the ink swirls, spreads and fades. A small fluid simulation runs on the render thread only while there is ink; with none it draws nothing and costs nothing. Under reduced motion the ink stays where it lands and fades.
