Class Glow

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Bloom around the shape. The light is a tight Gaussian plus a wide one through a soft knee, so a tinted glow keeps its hue instead of clipping to white. Switching IsLit off cools the light (it shrinks, deepens and dims) instead of cutting it.

public sealed class Glow : FXLayer, INotifyPropertyChanged, IDataContextProvider, ILogical, IThemeVariantHost, IResourceHost, IResourceNode, IStyleHost, ISetLogicalParent, ISetInheritanceParent, ISupportInitialize, INamed
