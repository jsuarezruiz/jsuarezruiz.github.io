Class FXHost

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Hosts one child and draws FX layers around it: under the child (glows, pulses, fills) and over it (sheens, grain, ripples). The layers follow the child's shape: its bounds and corner radius, taken from CornerRadius or, when that is unset, from the child itself. Layers render on the compositor thread and only tick while something moves, and while the host can be seen: a hidden host, or one scrolled out of view, asks for no frames.

Light that spills past the child (a glow's reach) is drawn outside the host's bounds, so it is cut by any clipping ancestor: a ScrollViewer, a Border with ClipToBounds, and Fluent's ContentControl, which clips by default. Leave room around the host inside such containers, or set ClipToBounds="False" where that is safe.

RefreshMask()

Captures the child's pixels again for layers that follow the content (a mask glow, a mask shimmer). The host does this itself when the child's size or one of its own properties changes; call it after changing something deeper inside the child. While changes keep coming, captures are spaced about 100 ms apart, with one more once they stop.
