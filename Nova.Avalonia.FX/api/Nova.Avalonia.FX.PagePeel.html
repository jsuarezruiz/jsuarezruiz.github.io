Class PagePeel

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

Hovered, a corner of the element curls up like a sticker or a page and shows its back, with a shadow under the curl; it peels further as the pointer nears the corner and settles back when the pointer leaves. IsPeeled holds it peeled from code. While peeled the layer draws a capture of the content (the real content is hidden and still takes input).
