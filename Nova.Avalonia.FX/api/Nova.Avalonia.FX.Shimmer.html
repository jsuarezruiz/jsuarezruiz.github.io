Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX

Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

A sheen band sweeping across the shape: skeleton loaders and "Thinking…" surfaces. The band starts and ends off the shape, so it never cuts. Under reduced motion the surface breathes.
