Class PointerShadow

Namespace
Nova.Avalonia.FX
Assembly
Nova.Avalonia.FX.dll

The pointer is a light over the page and the content casts a soft shadow away from it, in the shape of the content's own pixels: further and softer the further the light is, up to MaxOffset. Within PointerReach of the content the light follows the pointer across the whole window, so every card on a page casts its shadow away from it; beyond that, or with no pointer, the light rests above the content and the shadow falls just below it.
