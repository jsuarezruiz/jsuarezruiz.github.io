Retro grid

RetroGrid paints a synthwave scene: a floor of glowing grid lines running toward a horizon, a gradient sky above it and a striped sun sitting on the horizon. Use it as the background of a hero section, a splash screen or a game menu. It's a fill, so it paints the host's shape under the child and your content sits on top of it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a retro grid

Put the host around the panel, and leave the panel without a background so the scene shows through it. The host's CornerRadius rounds the scene's corners.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="16">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:RetroGrid />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Grid Height="320">
            <TextBlock Text="NIGHT DRIVE" Foreground="White" FontSize="40" FontWeight="Black"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Center" Margin="0,40,0,0" />
        </Grid>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

The scene follows the panel's size: the horizon sits at a share of its height, and the sun's radius is a share of its shorter side. CellSize is the size of a grid cell at the front (bottom) edge, in DIPs.

Move the horizon and the sun

Horizon is where the horizon sits, as a share of the height from the top (0.55 by default). Lower values give more floor. SunSize sets the sun's radius as a share of the shape's shorter side, and ShowSun="False" leaves the sky empty. The sun shades from SunColor at the top to SunEdgeColor at the bottom.

<fx:RetroGrid Horizon="0.4" SunSize="0.2" SunColor="#7DF9FF" SunEdgeColor="#3A86FF" />

<fx:RetroGrid ShowSun="False" CellSize="24" />

Change the colors

SkyColor is the top of the sky and HorizonColor the sky at the horizon, which also tints the haze along it. FloorColor fills the floor between the lines, and LineColor colors the lines. Glow sets the glow around the lines and the sun (1 by default, 0 for none, up to 2), LineWidth the lines' width in DIPs, and Fog how far below the horizon the lines fade into the haze.

<fx:RetroGrid SkyColor="#020617" HorizonColor="#0EA5E9" FloorColor="#020617"
              LineColor="#38BDF8" SunColor="#E0F2FE" SunEdgeColor="#0284C7"
              Glow="0.6" Fog="0.8" />

Set the pace

FlowSpeed is how many rows run toward the viewer each second (0.6 by default). At 0 the whole scene stands still and asks for no frames. Speed, which every layer has, multiplies the clock of the whole scene, the sun's drifting stripes included.

To speed the floor up or slow it down while it runs, change Speed or FlowSpeed: neither makes the animation jump.

<fx:RetroGrid FlowSpeed="1.5" />
<fx:RetroGrid Speed="0.5" />

Fills run on one shared clock, so retro grids on different hosts, with the same settings, move their floors in step, whenever each host was created.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
SkyColor Color #0D0221 The sky's color at the top; it shades to HorizonColor toward the horizon.
HorizonColor Color #FF2E88 The sky's color at the horizon, and the glow along the horizon.
FloorColor Color #0D0221 The floor's color under the grid lines.
LineColor Color #FF3CD6 The grid lines' color.
SunColor Color #FFD319 The sun's color at its top.
SunEdgeColor Color #FF2975 The sun's color at its bottom: it shades from SunColor at the top to this.
Horizon double 0.55 Where the horizon sits, as a share of the height from the top. Range 0.1–0.9 (10–90%).
CellSize double 36 Size of a grid cell at the front edge. Range 8–200 DIPs.
FlowSpeed double 0.6 Rows per second running toward the viewer. Range 0–4.
LineWidth double 1.5 Width of the grid lines, in DIPs. Range 0.5–6.
ShowSun bool true Whether the striped sun shows above the horizon.
SunSize double 0.28 The sun's radius, as a share of the shape's shorter side. Range 0.05–0.5 (5–50%).
Glow double 1 The glow around the grid lines and the sun, a multiple: 1 is the default, 0 none. Range 0–2.
Fog double 0.5 How far up the floor the lines fade into the haze at the horizon; more fades them from further forward. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a fill, Intensity is its coverage, up to 1.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces: the scene is opaque and paints its own colors, so it looks the same on a light or a dark surface.
  • Reduced motion: with FXSettings.ReducedMotion on, the floor and the sun hold one fixed pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: about 0.05 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark's 340×190 card, among the cheapest layers. The cost grows with the area. See Performance.
  • Seed: the grid has no randomness, so Seed changes nothing.
  • Bands and masks: like every fill, it has BorderWidth, which paints only a band inside the edge, and UseMask, which paints only the child's own pixels, such as large text. See Content masks.
