Glitch

Glitch breaks the content up like a failing digital signal: bands tear sideways, blocks jump, the color channels split, a few rows flash in the glitch colors and scanlines show, changing in steps about 20 times a second. Use a short burst for an error or a denied action, or let it glitch now and then on its own for a "signal lost" screen. It draws over the child and works on what is drawn there, and draws nothing between glitches.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a glitch

Wrap the content in a host. By default a press plays a short burst (TriggerOnPress is on):

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="12">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Glitch />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Button Content="Delete account" Padding="20,10" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Glitch on an error

Turn presses off and call Trigger() when something fails. A burst covers the whole content, so it takes no point:

<fx:Glitch x:Name="PasswordGlitch" TriggerOnPress="False" Duration="0.6" />

if (!await SignInAsync())
    PasswordGlitch.Trigger();

Glitch now and then

With IsContinuous, it glitches on its own, Frequency times a second on average. Duration and TriggerOnPress apply to bursts only:

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="12">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Glitch IsContinuous="True" Frequency="0.8" Displacement="18" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="230" Height="70" CornerRadius="12" Background="#0D0F14">
        <TextBlock Text="SIGNAL LOST" Foreground="White" FontWeight="Bold" FontSize="20"
                   HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
    </Border>
</fx:FXHost>

Displacement is how far bands tear sideways, Chroma how far the channels split, BlockSize the height of the thinnest band, and Color and SecondColor the colors of the flashing rows.

Methods

Call Trigger to play a burst without a press. See One-shots.

Method Description
Trigger() Plays a burst now (over the whole content, so it takes no point).

Properties

Property Type Default Description
IsContinuous bool false Glitches now and then on its own, Frequency times a second on average. Off (the default): only bursts.
Displacement double 16 How far bands tear sideways at full strength, DIPs. Range 0–80.
Chroma double 3 How far the color channels split, DIPs. Range 0–12.
BlockSize double 5 Height of the thinnest torn band, DIPs. Range 1–40.
Duration double 0.45 How long a burst lasts. Range 0.1–2 seconds. Applies when IsContinuous is false.
Frequency double 0.5 Glitches a second, on average. Range 0.05–4 per second. Applies when IsContinuous is true.
Color Color #FF2BD6 One of the two colors thin rows flash in during a glitch.
SecondColor Color #00E5FF The other color thin rows flash in; each row takes one of the two.
TriggerOnPress bool true Bursts when the host is pressed. Applies when IsContinuous is false.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • It tears what is drawn before it. It copies what is under it just before it draws (see Layers that read the backdrop): the child, and whatever shows through it. Inside an offscreen layer (an ancestor with Opacity below 1, an effect, or a clip that needs a layer) the copy can't see that ancestor's content, so keep such ancestors opaque.
  • It draws past the sides. Bands tear sideways past the left and right edges, by up to one and a half times Displacement plus Chroma. A clipping ancestor cuts them: leave room at the sides.
  • A press must land on the host's child. A child with no background in empty areas doesn't take presses: give it a Background, even Transparent.
  • Light and dark surfaces: it draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: a burst is a short, mild color split, and a continuous glitch stays still.
  • Cost: about 0.11 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark card while it glitches, and nothing between glitches. See Performance.
