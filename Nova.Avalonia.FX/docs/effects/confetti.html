Confetti

Confetti fires a burst of paper pieces that fly, tumble, flutter and fall: for a success, a completed order or a milestone. It is a one-shot: it draws nothing until you fire it, from code or on a press, and each burst is gone after its Duration. The pieces are drawn over the host and fly well past it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add confetti

With FireOnPress, a press on the host fires a burst from the press point:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14" HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Confetti FireOnPress="True" Count="80" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Button Content="Complete order" Padding="24,12" CornerRadius="14" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

The host hears the press even when the button handles it, so the button's Click still runs.

Styles

Style sets where the pieces come from. Shape picks the pieces themselves: Mixed (the default), Paper or Dots.

Value Description
Burst (default) A cone of pieces from a point (the press point, or the shape's center).
Cannons Two cannons at the shape's bottom corners, firing up and inward.
Fall Pieces drifting down from above the shape.

Fire from code

Name the layer and call Fire() when the moment comes, for example from an event your view model raises:

// In the view's code-behind; OrderPlaced is the view model's own event.
viewModel.OrderPlaced += (_, _) => Celebration.Fire();

Fire(at) takes a point in the host's coordinates. Only Burst uses it, or the center of the shape without one; Cannons always fire from the bottom corners, and Fall drops from above the shape. Bursts overlap: firing again while one plays starts another beside it, up to four at once, and a fifth drops the oldest.

Aim the burst

Angle points the burst (0 to the right, -90 up) and Spread sets the width of its cone. Power sets the launch speed and Gravity how fast the pieces fall. Colors takes your own palette:

<fx:Confetti x:Name="Celebration" Angle="-60" Spread="40" Power="1.4"
             Colors="#22d3ee, #a78bfa, #f472b6, #facc15" />

With Burst and Cannons, on a shape larger than about 250 × 250 DIPs, the whole burst grows up to 1.6 times as large, so it fills the shape. For a whole panel, try Cannons, or Fall for a gentle drift down.

Methods

Call Fire to start a burst from code.

Method Description
Fire(Point? at = null) Fires a burst from at (host coordinates), or the style's own origin.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Style ConfettiStyle Burst Where the pieces come from: a burst from a point, two cannons at the bottom corners, or a fall from above. Values: Burst, Cannons, Fall.
Colors FXColorList Unset The pieces' colors. Unset: six bright party colors.
Shape ConfettiShape Mixed The pieces' shape: paper strips, round dots, or a mix of both. Values: Mixed, Paper, Dots.
Count int 70 How many pieces each burst throws. Range 4–200.
Power double 1 Launch speed, as a multiple of a lively default. On a shape larger than about 250 × 250 DIPs the whole burst is up to 1.6 times as large (faster, with gravity to match), so it fills the shape. Range 0.2–3. Applies to the Burst and Cannons styles.
Spread double 75 Width of the burst's cone, degrees, centered on Angle. Range 0–360. Applies to the Burst style.
Angle double -90 Where the burst points, degrees: 0 to the right, -90 up; positive turns clockwise. Range -180 to 180. Applies to the Burst style.
Gravity double 1 How strongly the pieces fall, a multiple of the default (0: they don't fall). Range 0–3.
Size double 8 Size of a piece, DIPs. Range 2–24.
Duration double 2.6 How long a burst lasts. Range 0.5–6 seconds.
FireOnPress bool false Fires wherever the pointer is pressed on the host.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. While IsActive is off, presses fire nothing.

Notes

  • Light and dark. The pieces are drawn the same on both surfaces, in their own colors.
  • Reduced motion. The pieces appear in place and fade, without flying. See the poses.
  • Cost. Confetti is drawn as Skia shapes, not a shader: 0.1–0.5 ms of CPU time a frame while a burst plays, with almost no GPU time, and nothing between bursts. RenderScale doesn't apply. See What a layer costs.
  • Room to fly. Pieces fly well past the host. A clipping ancestor (a ScrollViewer, a Border with ClipToBounds) cuts them, and later siblings cover them: leave room, and give the host a higher ZIndex if needed.
