Ambient glow

Ambient glow lights the space behind the host's child with the child's own colors, like a screen's ambient light on the wall behind it. The host captures the child's pixels in color, and the layer blurs them, spreads them out from the center and draws them under the child. Use it behind album art, a video frame, a photo or a colorful card, so the color spills softly onto the page.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add an ambient glow

Give it something colorful to take its colors from. It has no color of its own.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost Margin="60">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:AmbientGlow />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Image Source="/Assets/album-cover.jpg" Width="200" Height="200" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Shape the light

Radius sets how soft the blur is. Spread scales the colors up from the content's center, so they reach further past its edge. Saturation and Brightness make the light more or less vivid.

<fx:AmbientGlow Radius="48" Spread="1.25" Saturation="1.6" Brightness="1.1" />

A larger Spread reaches further on a larger child, since it scales the content's own size.

Follow content that changes

The host captures the child again when its size or one of its own properties changes, such as an Image's Source. It doesn't watch the child's descendants: after changing something deeper inside, call RefreshMask() on the host. Behind a video, call it as the frames change.

// PlayerHost is the FXHost around the video surface.
private void OnFrameRendered(object? sender, EventArgs e)
{
    PlayerHost.RefreshMask();
}

While changes keep coming, captures are spaced about 100 ms apart, with one more once they stop. See Content masks.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Radius double 36 How far the colors blur, in DIPs; larger is softer and reaches further. Range 4–120.
Spread double 1.12 How far the colors spread past the content, as a scale from its center. Range 1–1.6.
Saturation double 1.35 How vivid the colors are: 0 gray, 1 the content's own, above 1 richer. Range 0–2.5.
Brightness double 0.9 How bright the glow is: 0 none, 1 the content's own brightness. Range 0–2.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On an ambient glow, Intensity dims the layer; Speed and Seed don't apply, since nothing moves.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the colors add light. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. Nothing changes: the glow is still.
  • Cost. The layer is still, so it draws when the content or a setting changes and asks for no frames in between. A redraw takes about 0.14 ms of GPU time on the bench's 340×190 card. Each capture renders the child to a bitmap on the UI thread. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. The light reaches past the content by about 2.5 × Radius, plus what Spread adds. A clipping ancestor cuts it off: leave room around the host.
  • Limits. Very large content is captured at a lower scale and gets a softer mask. If the child can't be captured, the host logs a warning and the layer draws nothing. See Content masks.
