Beam

Beam sends light along a curve from one point to another, over a faint track: pulses with a tail, marching dashes, or a steady shimmer. Use it to show data flowing between controls in a diagram, an integrations page, a sync screen or a pipeline. Its ends are points of the host's shape, or the centers of two controls inside the host, which it follows as the layout changes. It draws under the child, so the controls it connects sit on top of it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Connect two controls

Put the host around the panel that holds both controls, and point FromElement and ToElement at them. The beam runs between their centers, under them, and moves with them when the layout changes.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Beam FromElement="{Binding #AppNode}" ToElement="{Binding #ApiNode}" Curvature="0.2" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Grid ColumnDefinitions="Auto,*,Auto" Width="480" Height="160">
            <Border x:Name="AppNode" Grid.Column="0" Width="120" Height="72" CornerRadius="12"
                    Background="White" BorderBrush="#E4E4E7" BorderThickness="1">
                <TextBlock Text="App" FontWeight="SemiBold" Margin="14,12" />
            </Border>
            <Border x:Name="ApiNode" Grid.Column="2" Width="120" Height="72" CornerRadius="12"
                    Background="White" BorderBrush="#E4E4E7" BorderThickness="1">
                <TextBlock Text="API" FontWeight="SemiBold" Margin="14,12" />
            </Border>
        </Grid>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

The controls must be inside the host. You can also set them from code: SyncBeam.FromElement = AppNode;.

Without controls, From and To place the ends as shares of the shape, from 0,0 at the top left to 1,1 at the bottom right:

<fx:Beam From="0.1,0.8" To="0.9,0.2" />

Styles

Duration is the time a pulse, a dash or the shimmer takes to cross the path, in every style.

Value Description
Pulse (default) Comets with a tail, leaving every Interval seconds or on Send.
Dashes Dashes marching along the path.
Flow A steady shimmer running along the path. 
<fx:Beam Style="Dashes" DashLength="8" Duration="1.5" />

Send a pulse

In the Pulse style, a pulse leaves every Interval seconds. Set Interval to 0 and call Send() to send one only when something happens, such as a request going out. PulseLength sets the tail, as a share of the path.

<fx:Beam x:Name="RequestBeam" Interval="0" PulseLength="0.2" />

private void OnRequestSent(object? sender, EventArgs e)
{
    RequestBeam.Send();
}

Bow and direction

Curvature bows the path to the side: 0 is straight, and at 1 the middle stands a quarter of the path's length off the straight line. Positive values bow to the left of the way the light travels (up, when it runs from left to right). IsReversed runs the light from To back to From. Color is a pulse's bright head and TailColor its tail; TrackColor and Track set the faint path under it.

<fx:Beam Curvature="-0.4" IsReversed="True" Color="#34D399" TailColor="#22D3EE" Track="0.15" />

Methods

Method Description
Send() Sends one pulse along the path now (the Pulse style).

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Style BeamStyle Pulse How the light travels: comets with a tail, marching dashes, or a steady shimmer. Values: Pulse, Dashes, Flow.
From Point 0.08,0.5 Where the beam starts, as a share of the shape (0..1 each way).
To Point 0.92,0.5 Where the beam ends, as a share of the shape (0..1 each way).
Curvature double 0.25 How far the path bows to the side: 0 straight; at 1 its middle stands a quarter of its length off the straight line. Positive bows to the left of the way it travels (up, from left to right), negative the other way. Range -1 to 1.
Color Color #22D3EE A pulse's bright head, and the end of the path the light runs toward.
TailColor Color #A78BFA A pulse's tail, and the end of the path the light leaves from.
Width double 2 Thickness of the beam's line, in DIPs; the glow around it grows with it. Range 0.5–8.
Glow double 0.6 The soft light around the beam, as a share (0: none). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
TrackColor Color #9CA3AF The color of the faint path under the light; its alpha scales the path along with Track.
Track double 0.25 How much the path itself shows when no light is on it. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Duration double 2.5 Seconds a pulse or a dash takes to cross the path; the shimmer moves one 80-DIP wave along it in this time. Range 0.3–10.
Interval double 2.5 Seconds between pulses sent on their own; 0: only those sent with Send. Range 0–10. Applies to the Pulse style.
PulseLength double 0.3 A pulse's tail, as a share of the path. Range 0.05–1 (5–100%). Applies to the Pulse style.
DashLength double 10 Length of a dash, in DIPs; the gaps between dashes are as long. Range 2–60. Applies to the Dashes style.
IsReversed bool false Runs from To back to From.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a beam, Intensity dims the layer and Seed doesn't apply.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the light adds to what is beneath it. On a light surface it draws as tinted haze. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The beam holds a still gradient along the path. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. About 0.02 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench. The layer only shades the curve and its glow, not the whole host. A Pulse beam with Interval at 0 asks for frames only while a pulse is on the path. See Performance.
  • Many pulses. The path shows up to eight pulses at a time. With more (a short Interval), the newest are kept, so a pulse sent with Send() always shows.
  • Several beams. Put several Beam layers in one host to draw a diagram, one per connection.
