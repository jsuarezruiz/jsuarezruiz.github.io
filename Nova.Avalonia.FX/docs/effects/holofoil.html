Holo foil

Holographic foil laid over the shape: rainbow bands that shift as the pointer moves across it, as if the foil tilted, with fine etched lines, glitter that flares as the angle passes each fleck, and a soft glare under the pointer. Use it for collectible cards, premium badges, membership passes and rare items. Unlike other fills, it goes over the host's child, so the content shows through the foil. At rest it holds still and costs nothing.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a holo foil

Add a HoloFoil over a card, then move the pointer across it:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="16">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:HoloFoil />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="150" Height="200" CornerRadius="16" Background="#1D2140" Padding="14">
            <StackPanel VerticalAlignment="Bottom" Spacing="2">
                <TextBlock Text="RARE" FontSize="11" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="#C7D2FE" />
                <TextBlock Text="Foil card" FontSize="17" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="White" />
            </StackPanel>
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

The foil follows the pointer while it is over the shape, and settles back when it leaves.

Tilt it from code

PointAt tilts the foil toward a point in host coordinates, as if the pointer were there: for keyboard focus, a tour or an attract loop. Pass null to let it settle. The pointer takes over again when it moves.

// CardFoil is the HoloFoil (x:Name="CardFoil") in the host named CardHost.
CardFoil.PointAt(new Point(CardHost.Bounds.Width, 0)); // toward the top-right corner
CardFoil.PointAt(null);

Let it drift

Without a pointer, on touch screens or in a list nobody hovers, Drift moves the bands on their own, in bands per second:

<fx:HoloFoil Drift="0.1" />

With Drift at 0 (the default), the foil only moves with the pointer or PointAt.

Make holographic text and icons

With UseMask, the foil covers only the child's own pixels:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:HoloFoil UseMask="True" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="LEGENDARY" FontSize="32" FontWeight="Black" Foreground="#6366F1" />
</fx:FXHost>

The foil tints the letters rather than replacing them, so give the text a color that already stands out on its background.

Tune the foil

Strength sets how strongly the rainbow tints what is under it, and Band the width of one rainbow band. Angle turns the way the rainbow runs, and Tilt sets how far the bands shift as the pointer crosses the shape. Glare, Glitter and Lines set the glare under the pointer, the flecks and the etched lines. On small controls, where the label is most of the shape, a fainter foil keeps the label readable:

<fx:HoloFoil Strength="0.12" Glare="0.15" />

Methods

Call PointAt to aim the foil without a pointer, and with null to release it.

Method Description
PointAt(Point? at) Tilts the foil toward at (host coordinates); null settles it.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Strength double 0.24 How strongly the rainbow tints what is under it; on a dark surface, how much light it adds. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Band double 140 Width of one rainbow band. Range 8–600 DIPs.
Angle double 35 The way the rainbow runs, across its bands, degrees: 0 left to right (upright bands); positive turns clockwise. Range -180 to 180.
Tilt double 1 How far the bands shift as the pointer crosses the shape. Range 0–3.
Glare double 0.35 Strength of the soft glare under the pointer (0: none). Range 0–1.5.
Glitter double 0.6 How bright the glitter flares as the angle passes each fleck (0: none). Range 0–2.
Lines double 0.4 Etched lines across the bands. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Drift double 0 Bands per second drifting on their own (0: still until the pointer moves). Range 0–1.
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces: on a light surface the bands are pastel colors that tint the content; on a dark one they are a thin film's cooler colors, cyan through violet to pink, added as light. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Pointer: the foil reacts while the pointer is over the shape. See Layers that follow the pointer.
  • Reduced motion: the drift holds still, and the foil jumps to the pointer instead of easing toward it.
  • Intensity is the foil's coverage, from 0 to 1. Speed doesn't change it; the foil moves with the pointer, and Drift sets its own pace.
  • Cost: 0.060 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench, and nothing while it rests.
