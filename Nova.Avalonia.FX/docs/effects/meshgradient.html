Mesh gradient

A soft, living gradient: up to six colors, each pinned to its own point, blended smoothly into each other as the points drift slowly. The colors blend in OKLab, so the mixes between them stay clean instead of going muddy. Use it for hero cards, avatars, app backgrounds and gradient text. It is a fill: it paints the host's shape and goes under the child, or paints the child's own pixels with UseMask.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a mesh gradient

The fill goes under the child, so leave the child without a background:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:MeshGradient Preset="Dusk" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="260" Height="116" CornerRadius="16" Padding="16">
            <TextBlock Text="Dusk" FontSize="19" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="White"
                       VerticalAlignment="Bottom" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

Each preset brings a palette and sets Sharpness, Drift and Warp. Set any of them yourself to adjust a preset.

Value Description
Dusk (default) Deep indigo and violet with pink, orange and gold, like a sunset sky.
Lagoon Deep blue, teal and pale aqua, like shallow tropical water.
Orchid Six soft pinks, mauves, peach and slate blue, with crisper blends.
Graphite Near-black and slate grays that drift less.

Use your own colors

Colors takes up to six colors, separated by commas, semicolons or spaces. Set, it replaces the preset's palette:

<fx:MeshGradient Colors="#0B3D5C, #2EC4B6, #CBF3F0, #1B6CA8" />

Each color has a place in the shape, which its point drifts around: the first near the top-left corner, the second near the top-right, the third in the middle, the fourth near the bottom-left, the fifth near the bottom-right, and the sixth above and right of the middle. The points sit at fractions of the shape, so the gradient stretches with the host.

From code, set an FXColorList:

// Hero is the MeshGradient (x:Name="Hero"). Set Colors to null to go back to the preset's palette.
Hero.Colors = FXColorList.Parse("#7c3aed, #ec4899, #f59e0b");

Make gradient text

With UseMask, the gradient paints only the letters and goes over them. Choose colors that stand out from the background behind the text:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:MeshGradient UseMask="True" Colors="#7C3AED, #EC4899, #F59E0B, #22D3EE" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="Aurora" FontSize="46" FontWeight="Black" Foreground="White" />
</fx:FXHost>

After changing something deeper inside the child, call FXHost.RefreshMask(); see Content masks.

Make avatars and borders

A CornerRadius of 999 on the host makes a circle. BorderWidth paints only a band inside the edge, for a gradient ring or border:

<StackPanel Orientation="Horizontal" Spacing="14">
    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="999">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers><fx:MeshGradient Preset="Lagoon" /></fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="80" Height="80" />
    </fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="999">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers><fx:MeshGradient Preset="Orchid" BorderWidth="4" /></fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="80" Height="80" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</StackPanel>

Shape the blend

Drift sets how far the points wander from their places, as a share of the shape, and FlowSpeed how fast. Sharpness goes from wide, soft blends (1) to colors that keep to their own regions (higher). Warp bends the whole field gently with noise:

<fx:MeshGradient Preset="Orchid" Drift="0.2" FlowSpeed="0.1" Sharpness="3" Warp="0.8" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset MeshGradientPreset Dusk The palette used while Colors is unset, with matching Sharpness, Drift and Warp. Local values win. Values: Dusk, Lagoon, Orchid, Graphite.
Colors FXColorList Unset The colors (up to six). Unset: the preset's palette.
Drift double 0.12 How far the points wander from rest, as a fraction of the rect. Range 0–0.5 (0–50%).
FlowSpeed double 0.25 How fast the points drift and the warp moves (0: still). Range 0–2 per second.
Sharpness double 1.6 1: wide soft blends; higher keeps each color to its own region. Range 0.5–6.
Warp double 0.35 A gentle noise warp of the whole field. Range 0–2.
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Intensity is the fill's coverage, from 0 to 1, so a transition on it fades the gradient in and out. Seed changes how the points wander.
  • Still gradients: with FlowSpeed at 0, or both Drift and Warp at 0, the gradient holds still and requests no frames.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the gradient draws the same on both. Pick the text color by the color nearest the text.
  • Reduced motion: the gradient holds one pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.103 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench. See Performance.
  • Fills share one clock, so equal gradients on different hosts move in step.
