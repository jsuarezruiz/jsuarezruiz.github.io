Topography

Topography draws the contour lines of a slowly shifting landscape, like a topographic map: thin lines of even width at any slope, with every fifth one bolder. It's a calm background for a hero, an empty state or an outdoors app. It draws under the child, and by default only the lines, so whatever is behind the host shows between them.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add topography

Put the host around the panel, and leave the panel without a background so the lines show through it. A small plate behind the label keeps it readable over the lines:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Topography Scale="110" Levels="12" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Height="220" CornerRadius="14">
            <Border Background="#F1F5F9" CornerRadius="10" Padding="14,6"
                    HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center">
                <TextBlock Text="Trailheads" Foreground="#0F172A" FontWeight="SemiBold" />
            </Border>
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Set the lines

Levels is how many levels the landscape is cut into: more levels give more, closer lines (14 by default). Scale is the size of the hills in DIPs (180 by default), and LineWidth the width of a line in DIPs; every fifth line, the index line, is drawn bolder. A line keeps its width on steep and gentle slopes alike, and where lines crowd closer than a pixel they fade instead of filling in.

<fx:Topography Levels="20" Scale="260" LineWidth="0.75" />

The landscape shifts slowly on its own. Speed multiplies its clock; at 0 it stops. Unlike the fills, its clock starts when it first draws, so topography on different hosts doesn't move in step.

Color it for each theme

LineColor colors the lines (#94A3B8 by default), and BackgroundColor fills behind them (transparent by default). Pale lines suit a dark theme; the gallery draws them in a deeper slate (#64748B) in light themes. Define a color for each theme in ThemeDictionaries, as on the Weather page, and the lines change color when the theme does:

<fx:Topography LineColor="{DynamicResource ContourColor}" />

A map on paper, with its own background:

<fx:Topography LineColor="#8A6F4D" BackgroundColor="#F5F0E6" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
LineColor Color #94A3B8 The contour lines' color; its alpha is their opacity.
BackgroundColor Color #00FFFFFF Behind the lines; transparent shows the content.
Levels double 14 How many levels the landscape is cut into: more, closer lines. Range 3–40.
Scale double 180 The size of the hills. Range 40–800 DIPs.
LineWidth double 1 Width of a contour line, in DIPs; every fifth line is 1.8 times as wide. Range 0.25–4.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. Seed picks the landscape.

Notes

  • Not a fill: topography has no BorderWidth or UseMask, and it runs on its own clock.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the lines are drawn in LineColor on any surface, so pick a color for each theme, as above. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion: the landscape holds still. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.36 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark's 340×190 card, among the heavier backgrounds: it works out the landscape's height three times a pixel to find the slope. The cost grows with the area. RenderScale lowers it, but softens the thin lines. See Performance.
