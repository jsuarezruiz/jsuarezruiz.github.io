Liquid progress

Liquid progress fills the host's shape with a liquid up to Level: for progress bars, gauges, and battery or storage levels. Its surface waves and bubbles rise through it, and a change of level eases in with a little overshoot and sloshes the surface. It is a fill, drawn under the child as its background, rising from the bottom or filling from the left.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add liquid progress

Bind Level, from 0 (empty) to 1 (full). Here a slider fills a tall glass:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <StackPanel Orientation="Horizontal" Spacing="18" HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center">
        <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="18">
            <fx:FXHost.Layers>
                <fx:LiquidProgress Level="{Binding #LevelSlider.Value}" />
            </fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <Border Width="74" Height="150" CornerRadius="18" BorderBrush="#5a6170" BorderThickness="1" />
        </fx:FXHost>
        <Slider x:Name="LevelSlider" Orientation="Vertical" Height="150" Minimum="0" Maximum="1" Value="0.6" />
    </StackPanel>
</UserControl>

The liquid goes under the child, so the child has no background of its own: only a border line, and whatever sits on top of the liquid.

Show progress

Bind Level to a progress value from 0 to 1; values outside that range are clamped. Each new value eases in with a small overshoot, and Slosh sets how much the change stirs the surface (0: not at all).

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="22">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:LiquidProgress Level="{Binding UploadProgress}" Orientation="Horizontal" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="240" Height="44" CornerRadius="22" BorderBrush="#5a6170" BorderThickness="1">
        <TextBlock Text="{Binding UploadStatus}" Foreground="White" FontWeight="SemiBold"
                   HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
    </Border>
</fx:FXHost>

Orientation="Horizontal" fills from the left, for a bar; the default, Vertical, rises from the bottom. If your progress counts to 100, divide it by 100 in the view model or a converter.

Color the liquid

Color is the liquid near its surface, DeepColor the color it shades into with depth, and SurfaceColor the bright line along the surface, the wave behind it and the bubbles' rims. WaveHeight and WaveLength shape the waves, WaveSpeed sets how many pass a second, and Bubbles how many rise.

<fx:LiquidProgress Level="0.42" Color="#10b981" DeepColor="#065f46" SurfaceColor="#6ee7b7"
                   WaveHeight="6" Bubbles="0.5" />

For a liquid that stays still while the level holds, set WaveSpeed and Bubbles to 0: the layer then asks for frames only while a new level settles.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Level double 0.6 How full, 0..1. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Color Color #3B82F6 The liquid's color near its surface; it shades toward DeepColor further down.
DeepColor Color #1E40AF The color the liquid shades to with depth.
SurfaceColor Color #93C5FD The color of the bright line along the surface, the bubbles' rims and the wave behind.
WaveHeight double 4 How tall the surface waves are, in DIPs (0: flat). A change of level makes them taller for a moment. Range 0–24.
WaveLength double 120 Distance between wave crests, in DIPs. Range 16–600.
WaveSpeed double 0.6 Waves per second (0: a still surface; with Bubbles at 0 too, no frames are drawn while the level holds). Range 0–3.
Bubbles double 0.25 How many bubbles rise: the share of narrow columns that carry one (0: none). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Slosh double 1 How much a change of level stirs the surface. Range 0–2.
Orientation Orientation Vertical Vertical rises from the bottom; horizontal fills from the left. Values: Horizontal, Vertical.
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a fill, Intensity is its coverage, up to 1.

Notes

  • Light and dark. On a light surface the wave behind the surface is drawn paler and see-through. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The level jumps to its new value, and the surface stays calm. See the poses.
  • Cost. About 0.06 ms of GPU time a frame on a card. See What a layer costs.
  • Shape. The liquid fills the child's bounds with the host's corner radius, so give the child the full size of the bar or the glass. BorderWidth fills only a band inside the edge, and UseMask fills the child's own pixels instead, such as the letters of a text; see Content masks.
