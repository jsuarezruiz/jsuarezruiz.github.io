Metaballs

Glossy blobs that drift on slow paths and merge where they meet, like a lava lamp, over a base color. Each blob takes one of the colors in turn and has a soft sheen along its rim. Use it for playful cards, music and media tiles, splash screens and ambient backgrounds. It is a fill: it paints the host's shape, background included, and goes under the child.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add metaballs

The fill goes under the child, so leave the child without a background:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Metaballs Preset="Lava" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="340" Height="190" CornerRadius="16" Padding="20">
            <TextBlock Text="Weekly report" FontSize="17" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="White" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

Each preset brings a palette and sets BackgroundColor: Lava is warm reds and oranges on a deep plum, Mint cool greens and cyans on a deep teal. Set either yourself to adjust a preset.

Value Description
Lava (default) Red, orange and hot-pink blobs on dark plum, like a lava lamp.
Mint Mint, aqua and lime blobs on a dark teal base.

Use your own colors

The blobs take the colors in Colors in turn, up to six, over BackgroundColor:

<fx:Metaballs Colors="#A78BFA, #F472B6, #38BDF8" BackgroundColor="#0F0A1F" />

Pick the text color by the background: light text over a dark BackgroundColor, dark text over a light one.

Shape the blobs

Count sets how many blobs there are, from 1 to 8. Size sets their size relative to the shape, so the blobs fill a small badge and a large panel alike: at 0.2 they cover about two fifths of the shape. Gloss is the sheen on the blobs, and FlowSpeed how fast they drift:

<fx:Metaballs Preset="Mint" Count="4" Size="0.3" Gloss="0.8" FlowSpeed="0.2" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset MetaballsPreset Lava The blob palette used while Colors is unset, with a matching BackgroundColor. Values: Lava, Mint.
Colors FXColorList Unset The colors (up to six). Unset: the preset's palette.
BackgroundColor Color #1A0F1F The base color behind the blobs.
Count int 6 How many blobs. Range 1–8.
Size double 0.2 Blob size, for any shape: the radius is 1.8 × Size times the side of the square each blob has to itself (the square root of the shape's area over the count), and at most half the shorter side. At 0.2 the blobs cover about two fifths of the shape. Range 0.05–0.5 (5–50%).
FlowSpeed double 0.4 How fast the blobs drift (0: still). Range 0–2 per second.
Gloss double 0.5 Sheen on the blobs. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Intensity is the fill's coverage, from 0 to 1, so a transition on it fades the fill in and out.
  • Still blobs: with FlowSpeed at 0, the blobs hold still and the layer requests no frames.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the blobs draw the same on both; BackgroundColor sets the base under them.
  • Reduced motion: the blobs hold one pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.097 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench. See Performance.
  • Like every fill, metaballs can paint a band inside the edge with BorderWidth, or only the child's own pixels with UseMask.
