Swirl

Colors stirred into a slow swirl: folded, flowing noise is twisted around the center of the shape and colored through a ramp of up to six colors, blended in OKLab. It looks like ink spreading in water or veined marble. Use it for hero cards, splash screens, cover art and gradient text. It is a fill: it paints the host's shape and goes under the child, or paints the child's own pixels with UseMask.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a swirl

The fill goes under the child, so leave the child without a background:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Swirl Preset="Ink" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="260" Height="116" CornerRadius="16" Padding="16">
            <TextBlock Text="Ink" FontSize="19" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="White"
                       VerticalAlignment="Bottom" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

Each preset brings a palette and sets Scale, Turns and Warp. Set any of them yourself to adjust a preset.

Value Description
Ink (default) Navy, indigo and cyan swirling into white, like ink in water.
Marble Cream with gray veins folded finely through it, like polished marble.

Use your own colors

Colors takes up to six colors, which form a ramp from the first to the last; the swirl maps its folds onto that ramp. Set, they replace the preset's palette:

<fx:Swirl Colors="#1A0B2E, #C2185B, #FF8A3D, #FFE1A8" />

Stir it

Turns is how far the colors twist around the center, in turns at the middle: positive values twist counterclockwise, negative ones clockwise, and 0 leaves the noise untwisted. Warp sets how much the noise folds into itself, Scale the size of its features, and FlowSpeed how fast it moves:

<fx:Swirl Preset="Marble" Turns="-0.8" Warp="2" Scale="120" FlowSpeed="0.1" />

Make swirled text

With UseMask, the swirl paints only the letters and goes over them. Choose colors that stand out from the background behind the text:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Swirl UseMask="True" Colors="#3B2C85, #2FB3C9, #7C3AED" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="Swirl" FontSize="46" FontWeight="Black" />
</fx:FXHost>

Fill a window

The swirl's cost grows with its area. On a large panel, shade it at half resolution:

<fx:Swirl Preset="Marble" RenderScale="0.5" />

On a 700×500 panel this took the swirl from 0.77 ms to 0.56 ms a frame in the gallery's bench.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset SwirlPreset Ink The palette used while Colors is unset, with matching Scale, Turns and Warp. Local values win. Values: Ink, Marble.
Colors FXColorList Unset The colors (up to six). Unset: the preset's palette.
Scale double 140 Feature size. Range 16–480 DIPs.
FlowSpeed double 0.2 How fast the colors stir (0: still). Range 0–2 per second.
Turns double 0.6 How far the colors twist around the center, in turns at the middle: positive counterclockwise, negative clockwise. Range -2 to 2.
Warp double 1 How much the noise folds into itself. Range 0–2.5.
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Intensity is the fill's coverage, from 0 to 1. Seed changes the pattern.
  • Still swirls: with FlowSpeed at 0, the swirl holds still and requests no frames.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the swirl draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the swirl holds one pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.311 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench, one of the heavier fills. See Render scale.
  • Fills share one clock, so equal swirls on different hosts move in step.
