Waves

Layered bands of color, one per color, stacked from back to front, with rolling, swaying crests; each band in front shades the one behind it. It reads as a sea, a range of dunes or hills. Use it for headers, weather and travel cards, footers and calm backgrounds. It is a fill: it paints the host's shape and goes under the child, or paints the child's own pixels with UseMask.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add waves

The fill goes under the child, so leave the child without a background:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Waves Preset="Sea" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="120" Height="116" CornerRadius="16" Padding="12">
            <TextBlock Text="Sea" FontSize="13" FontWeight="SemiBold" Foreground="#0A2649"
                       VerticalAlignment="Top" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

Each preset brings a palette and sets Amplitude, Crests and FlowSpeed. Set any of them yourself to adjust a preset.

Value Description
Sea (default) Pale sky blue at the back to deep navy in front, with lively crests.
Dunes Sand cream to deep rust, with broad, slow crests.

Use your own colors

Each color in Colors is one band, up to six. The first is the band at the back, at the top of the shape, and the last the one in front, at the bottom. A dusk sea:

<fx:Waves Colors="#FFD6A5, #F28482, #84A59D, #3D5A80, #1D2D44" />

Text sits on the band at its height, so pick its color by that band: dark text near the top of the Sea preset, light text lower down.

Shape the crests

Amplitude is the height of the crests, as a share of the shape, and Crests how many cross it. FlowSpeed sets how fast they roll, and Softness how soft the edges between bands are. Angle turns the direction the bands stack along; 0 is top to bottom, and positive angles turn clockwise:

<fx:Waves Preset="Dunes" Amplitude="0.1" Crests="2.5" Softness="0.02" Angle="90" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset WavesPreset Sea The palette used while Colors is unset, with matching Amplitude, Crests and FlowSpeed. Local values win. Values: Sea, Dunes.
Colors FXColorList Unset The colors (up to six). Unset: the preset's palette.
Amplitude double 0.05 Crest height, as a fraction of the rect. Range 0–0.25 (0–25%).
Crests double 1.6 Crests across the rect. Range 0.2–6.
FlowSpeed double 0.4 How fast the crests roll and sway (0: still). Range 0–2 per second.
Angle double 0 Direction the bands stack along, degrees: 0 top to bottom; positive turns clockwise. Range -180 to 180.
Softness double 0.006 Edge softness, as a fraction of the rect. Range 0–0.1 (0–10%).
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Intensity is the fill's coverage, from 0 to 1.
  • Still waves: with FlowSpeed or Amplitude at 0, the bands hold still and the layer requests no frames.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the waves draw the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the bands hold one pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.073 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench. See Performance.
  • Like every fill, waves can paint only the child's own pixels with UseMask, or a band inside the edge with BorderWidth.
