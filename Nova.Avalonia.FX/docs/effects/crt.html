CRT

Crt shows the content as if on an old cathode-ray tube: a barrel bulge, scanlines, a color split, an aperture grille, a soft glow around bright parts, a bright bar rolling down the screen and a darker rim. Use it for a retro game screen, a terminal, a "live feed" card or a playful error page. It draws over the child and works on what is drawn there: the child, and whatever shows through it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a CRT

Wrap the content in a host. A picture or a dark screen with bright text suits it:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="18">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Crt Bulge="0.12" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="220" Height="130" Padding="16,12" CornerRadius="18" Background="#0B120E">
            <TextBlock Text="CH 03" FontSize="15" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="#4ADE80" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Give it something to show

The tube copies what is under it just before it draws (see Layers that read the backdrop). Over the child, the default, that is the child and whatever shows through it. With a see-through child, the host becomes a screen over what is behind it, as on the gallery's home page, where a Border without a background shows a moving scene through the tube. Either way, something must be drawn there first: controls drawn after the host sit in front of it. Inside an offscreen layer (an ancestor with Opacity below 1, an effect, or a clip that needs a layer) the copy can't see that ancestor's content, so keep such ancestors opaque.

Tune the tube

Bulge sets the barrel bulge, Scanline the scanline period and ScanDepth how dark the lines are. Chroma is how far the color channels split, Glow the soft glow around bright parts, Vignette the darker rim and Roll the rolling bar:

<fx:Crt Bulge="0.2" Scanline="2" ScanDepth="0.4" Chroma="1.5" Vignette="0.8" />

Keep the picture still

The rolling bar is the only part that moves. With Roll="0" nothing moves, so the layer draws once and asks for no frames until what is under it changes:

<fx:Crt Roll="0" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Bulge double 0.08 How far the picture bulges like a curved tube: the middle swells and the corners curve away (0: flat). Range 0–0.4.
Scanline double 1.5 Scanline period. Range 1–8 DIPs.
ScanDepth double 0.28 How dark the scanlines are: 0 none, 1 black between the lines. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Chroma double 0.8 How far the color channels split, DIPs. Range 0–6.
Vignette double 0.6 How much the corners darken (0: none). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Glow double 0.35 The soft glow around bright parts, as a share (0: none). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Roll double 1 The bright bar rolling down the screen (0: none). Range 0–2.
Feather double 2 How soft the layer's edge is, in DIPs. Range 0–16.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. The tube has no randomness, so Seed doesn't apply; Speed sets the pace of the rolling bar.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces: it draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the bar stops rolling; the rest of the tube stays.
  • Cost: about 0.13 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark card. See Performance.
