Sparkles

Sparkles scatters crisp four-point stars around the host's shape, each with its own size, twinkle and spin. The stars take turns, so only a few are lit at once. Use them on a "New" badge, a premium feature or a reward. A burst pops every star at once and flings it out a little, as a one-shot for a success, a like or a completed task. The stars sit just outside the shape and draw over the child.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add sparkles

The defaults suit a small pill; on a larger target, raise Count and Size.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="999">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Sparkles Count="12" Size="12" Margin="14" Color="#F59E0B" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="92" Height="38" CornerRadius="19" Background="#FEF3C7">
            <TextBlock Text="New" Foreground="#B45309" FontWeight="SemiBold"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Margin is how far outside the shape the stars sit. Life is how long each star stays lit before it rests.

Burst on a press

Turn IsContinuous off to show stars only during a burst, and BurstOnPress on to burst whenever the host is pressed. Every burst has its own layout, so two quick presses show two different bursts.

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="10">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Sparkles IsContinuous="False" BurstOnPress="True"
                     Count="18" Size="10" Margin="22" Color="#7C5CFF" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Button Content="Like" CornerRadius="10" />
</fx:FXHost>

Burst from code

Name the layer and call Burst() when something worth celebrating happens, for example when a save completes:

<fx:Sparkles x:Name="SaveSparkles" IsContinuous="False" />

private void OnSaved(object? sender, EventArgs e)
{
    SaveSparkles.Burst();
}

A burst takes no point: the stars sit all around the shape.

Methods

Method Description
Burst() Pops every star once; they fly a little way out and twinkle away. The stars sit all around the shape, so a burst takes no point.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Color Color #FFE6A8 The stars' color.
Count int 6 How many stars sit around the shape; continuous stars rest between appearances, so fewer are lit at once. Range 1–32.
Life double 1.5 How long a star stays lit, seconds. Range 0.3–5.
Size double 7 A star's base size, DIPs: most are smaller, a few larger. Range 2–24.
Margin double 10 How far outside the shape stars sit. Range 0–60 DIPs.
IsContinuous bool true Twinkle forever. Off: stars show only during a Burst.
BurstOnPress bool false Bursts whenever the pointer is pressed on the host.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On sparkles, Intensity is the strength of the light, up to 3, and Seed picks where the stars sit.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the stars add light and their cores turn white. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze with cores in the layer's own color, so a pale gold reads weakly on white: pick a deeper color for light themes, such as amber. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. Continuous stars hold still: a fixed handful stays lit. A burst still shows, but the stars fade in and out in place without flying or spinning. See Reduced motion.
  • One-shots. With IsContinuous off, the layer draws nothing between bursts. When too many bursts are live at once, the oldest fades out. See One-shots.
  • Cost. About 0.19 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. The stars draw outside the shape, as far as Margin plus a star's size. A clipping ancestor cuts them off: leave room around the host.
