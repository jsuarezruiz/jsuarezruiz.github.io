Jelly

Jelly makes the element squash and wobble like jelly when pressed, then settle: a damped spring squashes it down and out and overshoots, while a bulge spreads from the press point. Use it for playful buttons, a "like" heart, toggles or game UI. While it wobbles, the layer draws a capture of the content in its place; the real content is hidden but still takes input, and it takes over again once the wobble settles.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add jelly

Wrap the control in a host. A press wobbles it from the press point (WobbleOnPress is on), and the control still gets its own click:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="26">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Jelly />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Button Content="Press me" Width="190" Height="52" CornerRadius="26" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Wobble from code

Wobble(at) wobbles from a point in the host's coordinates, or from the center with no point. Set WobbleOnPress="False" to wobble only from code, for example when a new message arrives:

InboxJelly.Wobble();

Make it softer or stiffer

Amount is how far it squashes at first, Frequency how many wobbles a second, Damping how fast it settles (the wobble shrinks by a factor of e every 1/Damping seconds), and Ripple the bulge that spreads from the press point:

<fx:Jelly Amount="0.3" Frequency="3" Damping="1.5" Ripple="0.8" />

Methods

Call Wobble to play the effect without a press. See One-shots.

Method Description
Wobble(Point? at = null) Wobbles now, from at (host coordinates) or the center.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Amount double 0.18 How far it squashes at first. Range 0–0.6 (0–60%).
Frequency double 4.5 Wobbles a second. Range 1–12 per second.
Damping double 2.5 How fast it settles: the wobble shrinks by e every 1/Damping seconds. Range 0.5–12.
Ripple double 0.5 The bulge that spreads from the press point. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
WobbleOnPress bool true Wobbles when the host is pressed.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. The wobble runs on its own spring, so Speed and Seed don't apply.

Notes

  • It stands in for the content. When a wobble starts, the layer captures the content as it looks then and hides the real element, which keeps its place and still takes input. A change to the content during the wobble shows once it settles. With nothing to capture (no content, invisible, or not laid out yet), or while the layer isn't active, it doesn't wobble. The same stand-in is described for the transitions in Show and hide transitions.
  • It doesn't read the backdrop. It works from its own capture of the content, so it needs nothing behind the host.
  • It draws past the shape. The squash stretches the element sideways, by up to about 0.8 of Amount times half its larger side, and the bulge adds a few DIPs. A clipping ancestor cuts it: leave room around the host.
  • A press must land on the host's child. A child with no background in empty areas doesn't take presses: give it a Background, even Transparent.
  • Light and dark surfaces: it draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: a small, quick squash.
  • Cost: under 0.1 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark card while it wobbles, and nothing between wobbles. See Performance.
