Pulse

Pulse sends light out from the host's shape. Radar rings, a breathing halo and a heartbeat run on their own and follow the shape's outline: use them for a live or recording status, an unread dot or a call to action. A ripple spreads from where the shape is tapped, inside it, like a touch response. A ping flies from the shape's edge to a point or another control, to show that something was sent.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a pulse

The default style is Radar. Here it pairs with a glow on a recording dot; CornerRadius="999" makes the host's shape a circle, so the rings stay round.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="999">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Pulse Style="Radar" Color="#F97316" Reach="16" Width="1.5" />
            <fx:Glow Color="#F97316" Radius="9" Hot="1" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Ellipse Width="20" Height="20" Fill="#F97316" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Count sets how many rings are out at once, Period how long each takes, and Reach how far they travel from the edge.

Styles

Radar, Breathe and Heartbeat draw under the child and run continuously. Ripple and Ping draw over it and play once each time they are fired.

Value Description
Radar (default) Rings that leave the shape's edge and fade as they spread.
Breathe A halo that swells and settles.
Heartbeat Lub-dub beats; each beat's ring keeps spreading under the next.
Ripple A one-shot ripple from a point, confined to the shape. Overlapping taps overlap.
Ping A comet that launches from the shape's edge, winds up, flies with a decelerating landing, then blooms and cools at its destination: Ping or PingTo.

Ripple on a tap

With Style="Ripple", every press on the host starts a ripple from the press point, because TriggerOnPress is on by default. Ripples overlap, so a quick double tap shows two. The press has to land on the host's content, so give it a background.

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Pulse Style="Ripple" Color="#3B82F6" Width="2.5" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="240" Height="96" CornerRadius="14" Background="White" Cursor="Hand">
        <TextBlock Text="Tap anywhere" HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
    </Border>
</fx:FXHost>

Unset, Reach lets a ripple spread to the farthest corner. Duration sets how long each ripple lives.

Send a ping

A ping winds up, flies from the shape's edge with a slowing landing, then blooms and cools where it lands. Call PingTo with the control it should land on, and give the sending host a higher ZIndex so the ping draws on top of the target:

<StackPanel Orientation="Horizontal" Spacing="100">
    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="999" ZIndex="1">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Pulse x:Name="Pinger" Style="Ping" TriggerOnPress="False" Color="#60A5FA" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Button Content="Send" Click="OnSend" />
    </fx:FXHost>
    <Border x:Name="Inbox" Width="120" Height="72" CornerRadius="12" Background="White" />
</StackPanel>

private void OnSend(object? sender, RoutedEventArgs e) => Pinger.PingTo(Inbox);

Without a destination, Ping() flies along Direction for Travel DIPs. Set Interval to send one every so many seconds on its own.

Methods

Call these to fire a ripple or a ping from code, for example from a command or a network event. Positions are in the host's coordinates.

Method Description
Trigger(Point? at = null) Starts a ripple at at (host coordinates), or at the shape's center.
Ping(Point? to = null) Fires a ping from the shape's edge to to (host coordinates), or along Direction for Travel. Pings overlap. Style must be Ping.
PingTo(Control target) Fires a ping from the shape's edge to the center of target.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Style PulseStyle Radar The kind of pulse: radar rings, a breathing halo, a heartbeat, tap ripples, or pings that fly to a destination. Values: Radar, Breathe, Heartbeat, Ripple, Ping.
Color Color #3B82F6 The color of the rings, the halo and the pings.
CoreColor Color #E8F1FF The white-hot color where the light is brightest: the halo near the edge, a ripple's ring, a ping's head. Applies to the Breathe, Heartbeat, Ripple, and Ping styles.
Count int 3 How many radar rings are out at once. Range 1–8. Applies to the Radar style.
Period double 2.4 Seconds per cycle: one ring's trip, one breath, or one heartbeat. Range 0.3–8. Applies to the Radar, Breathe, and Heartbeat styles.
Reach double 36 How far rings travel from the edge. For ripples, unset means the farthest corner. Range 4–160 DIPs. Applies to the Radar, Breathe, Heartbeat, and Ripple styles.
Width double 1.6 Thickness of the rings, in DIPs; they widen as they spread. Range 0.3–8. Applies to the Radar, Heartbeat, and Ripple styles.
Duration double 0.75 A ripple's life, or a ping's flight to its destination (it then blooms and cools for a while longer), in seconds. Range 0.1–3. Applies to the Ripple and Ping styles.
Occlusion double 0.15 How much the light also covers what is behind it. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Direction double 0 Ping: the direction a ping flies when no destination is given, degrees: 0 to the right, 90 down; positive turns clockwise. Range -180 to 180. Applies to the Ping style.
Travel double 160 Ping: how far a ping flies when no destination is given. Range 8–600 DIPs. Applies to the Ping style.
Lag double 0.16 Ping: how far the tail trails the head, as a share of the flight. Range 0–0.5 (0–50%). Applies to the Ping style.
HeadRadius double 2.6 Ping: radius of the comet's head, in DIPs; it swells as it blooms at the destination. Range 0.5–12. Applies to the Ping style.
Interval double 0 Ping: fire one every this many seconds (0: only when asked). Range 0–10. Applies to the Ping style.
TriggerOnPress bool true Starts a ripple (or fires a ping) whenever the pointer is pressed on the host; on by default. The other styles run on their own and ignore presses. Applies to the Ripple and Ping styles.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a pulse, Intensity is the strength of the light, up to 3.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the rings add light. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze, and the radar turns down its edge glow and the wash between rings, so the rings carry the effect. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The rings become a static halo. A ripple is a faint flash over the shape, and a ping glows up and cools at its destination without flying. See Reduced motion.
  • One-shots. Ripples and pings draw nothing between shots. When too many are live at once, the oldest fades out. See One-shots.
  • Cost. About 0.1 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. Radar, breathe and heartbeat rings travel Reach DIPs past the shape, and a ping flies past it. A clipping ancestor, such as a ScrollViewer, cuts them off: leave room around the host.
