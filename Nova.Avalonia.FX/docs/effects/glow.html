Glow

Glow puts a bloom of light around the host's shape: a soft halo, a neon tube with a white-hot rim, or an aura of three colors flowing around the border. Use it to mark a primary action, an online status, a focused input or a prompt box. It draws under the child and follows its bounds and corner radius; the Mask style follows the child's own pixels instead, so text and icons glow letter by letter. Switched off, the light cools instead of cutting out.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a glow

Put a Glow in the host's layers and give it a color. The host takes the corner radius from the child, so the light follows the button's rounded corners.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Glow Color="#7C5CFF" Radius="16" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Button Content="Generate" CornerRadius="24" Padding="40,12" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Radius sets the size of the bloom and Reach how far it fades out, in multiples of the radius. Hot sets how much of the white-hot CoreColor shows where the light is strongest.

Styles

Neon and Aura add a thin rim of light, as wide as RimWidth. The aura flows through Color, CoreColor and ThirdColor at AuraSpeed.

Value Description
Soft (default) Soft bloom around the shape.
Neon A white-hot hairline rim with colored bloom on both sides.
Aura A three-color spectrum flowing around the perimeter, with an inner rim light.
Mask Bloom around the child's own pixels (text, icons) instead of its box. 
<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Glow Style="Aura" Color="#7C5CFF" CoreColor="#EC4899" ThirdColor="#22D3EE"
                 Radius="12" Inner="0.35" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBox PlaceholderText="Ask anything…" Width="270" CornerRadius="22" />
</fx:FXHost>

Inner adds light inside the shape, toward its edge.

Switch the light on and off

Bind IsLit. When it turns off, the light shrinks, deepens and dims over Cool seconds; when it turns back on, it heats up over Attack seconds.

<StackPanel Spacing="12">
    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Glow Style="Neon" Color="#22D3EE" Cool="0.8"
                     IsLit="{Binding #OnlineSwitch.IsChecked}" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="200" Height="84" CornerRadius="12" Background="#15171C" />
    </fx:FXHost>
    <ToggleSwitch x:Name="OnlineSwitch" IsChecked="True" OnContent="Online" OffContent="Offline" />
</StackPanel>

Glow around text and icons

With Style="Mask", the light blooms around the child's own pixels rather than its box. Breathe makes it swell and settle every BreathePeriod seconds.

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Glow Style="Mask" Color="#F472B6" Radius="10" Breathe="0.4" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="LIVE" FontSize="32" FontWeight="Bold" Foreground="White" />
</fx:FXHost>

The host captures the child when it changes. After changing something deeper inside it, call RefreshMask() on the host. See Content masks.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Style GlowStyle Soft How the light is drawn: a soft bloom, a neon rim, a flowing aura, or a bloom around the child's own pixels. Values: Soft, Neon, Aura, Mask.
Color Color #7C5CFF The light's color.
CoreColor Color #F4F0FF The white-hot core color.
ThirdColor Color #22D3EE The aura's third color. Applies to the Aura style.
Radius double 14 How wide the bloom is, in DIPs; larger is softer and spreads further. Range 1–64.
Inner double 0 Light inside the shape, toward its edge. Range 0–1 (0–100%). Applies to the Soft, Neon, and Aura styles.
Occlusion double 0.15 How much the light also covers what is behind it. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Breathe double 0 Breathing amplitude (0 = steady). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
BreathePeriod double 2.6 Seconds per breath, when Breathe is above 0. Range 0.2–8.
Hot double 0.6 How much of the core color shows where the light is hottest. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
AuraSpeed double 0.12 How fast the aura's colors travel around the border, in turns per second. Range 0–1. Applies to the Aura style.
RimWidth double 0.6 Width of the bright hairline on the border, in DIPs. Range 0.1–4. Applies to the Neon and Aura styles.
Reach double 4.5 How far the light reaches, in multiples of the radius. Range 1–8. Applies to the Soft, Neon, and Aura styles.
IsLit bool true Switch the light on or off. Off, it cools over Cool seconds.
Attack double 0.12 Seconds the light takes to warm up when IsLit turns on. Range 0.01–2.
Cool double 0.6 How long the light takes to cool after IsLit turns off, in seconds: its heat falls to about a third in this time. Range 0.05–4.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a glow, Intensity is the strength of the light, up to 3.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the light adds to what is beneath it and its hottest parts turn white. On a light surface it draws as tinted haze in its own color. Very pale colors read weakly on white, so pick deeper colors for light themes. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The breathing and the aura's flow hold still. Switching IsLit off still cools the light, as a fade. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. About 0.06 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. A glow that doesn't breathe and isn't an aura draws once and then asks for no frames. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. The light reaches past the shape by about Radius × Reach. A clipping ancestor, such as a ScrollViewer or a ContentControl, cuts it off: leave room around the host.
