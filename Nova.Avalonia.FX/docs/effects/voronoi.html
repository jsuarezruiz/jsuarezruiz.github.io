Voronoi

A mosaic of cells around points that orbit slowly, so the cells shift and trade space with their neighbors. Each cell takes one of the colors and is shaded toward its rim, and thin seams run between them, like tiles, cracked glaze or stained glass. Use it for game and achievement cards, decorative headers and playful buttons. It is a fill: it paints the host's shape and goes under the child, or paints the child's own pixels with UseMask.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a Voronoi fill

The fill goes under the child, so leave the child without a background:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Voronoi Preset="Cells" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="120" Height="116" CornerRadius="16" Padding="12">
            <TextBlock Text="Cells" FontSize="13" FontWeight="SemiBold" Foreground="White"
                       VerticalAlignment="Bottom" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

Each preset brings a palette and sets SeamColor, CellSize, Seam and Depth: Cells is large tiles in shades of blue, Stained smaller cells of colored glass. Set any of them yourself to adjust a preset.

Value Description
Cells (default) Tiles in four shades of navy blue with dark seams.
Stained Small red, orange, purple, teal and lime panes with dark leading, like stained glass.

Use your own colors

Each cell takes one of the colors in Colors, up to six. Set, they replace the preset's palette:

<fx:Voronoi Colors="#FDE68A, #FCA5A5, #A7F3D0, #BFDBFE" SeamColor="#FFFFFF" Seam="2.5" />

Shape the cells

CellSize sets the size of the cells, in DIPs. Seam is the width of the seams between them (0 for none) and SeamColor their color. Depth shades each cell toward its rim; at 0 the cells are flat. FlowSpeed sets how fast the points orbit:

<fx:Voronoi Preset="Stained" CellSize="28" Seam="3" Depth="0" FlowSpeed="0.1" />

Make mosaic text

With UseMask, the cells fill only the letters and go over them. Large, bold type with small cells shows the pattern best:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Voronoi Preset="Stained" UseMask="True" CellSize="10" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="LEVEL UP" FontSize="44" FontWeight="Black" />
</fx:FXHost>

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset VoronoiPreset Cells The palette used while Colors is unset, with matching SeamColor, CellSize, Seam and Depth. Local values win. Values: Cells, Stained.
Colors FXColorList Unset The colors (up to six). Unset: the preset's palette.
SeamColor Color #0B0F1A The color of the seams between cells.
CellSize double 36 Average size of a cell, in DIPs. Range 6–200.
FlowSpeed double 0.25 How fast the seed points orbit, reshaping the cells (0: still). Range 0–2 per second.
Seam double 1.5 Seam width (0: none). Range 0–8 DIPs.
Depth double 0.4 Shading toward each cell's rim, as a share (0: flat cells). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Intensity is the fill's coverage, from 0 to 1. Seed changes the layout of the cells.
  • Still cells: with FlowSpeed at 0, the cells hold still and the layer requests no frames.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the cells draw the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the cells hold one pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.258 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench, one of the heavier fills. On a 700×500 panel it takes 0.76 ms, and 0.40 ms with RenderScale="0.5". See Render scale.
