Light rays

Light rays sends shafts of light across the host's shape from a source just outside it, like sun through a window: a fan of rays that shimmer and shift slowly, bright where they come in and fading with distance. Use it on a hero, a welcome card or a dark backdrop that should feel lit. It draws over the child, inside the shape, as added light.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add light rays

Put the layer over a darker surface or picture, where the light has something to show against. The host here uses the dark surface, so the rays add light even in a light theme.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14" Surface="Dark">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:LightRays Angle="50" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="236" Height="128" CornerRadius="14" Padding="18">
            <Border.Background>
                <LinearGradientBrush StartPoint="0%,0%" EndPoint="100%,100%">
                    <GradientStop Color="#0B1220" Offset="0" />
                    <GradientStop Color="#1E293B" Offset="1" />
                </LinearGradientBrush>
            </Border.Background>
            <TextBlock Text="Good morning" Foreground="White" FontWeight="Bold" FontSize="20"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Right" VerticalAlignment="Bottom" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Aim the light

Angle is the way the light travels, in degrees: 0 to the right, 90 down, with positive angles turning clockwise. The source sits on the opposite side, outside the shape, by Distance (a share of the shape's diagonal). The default, 60, falls from the top left toward the bottom right.

<!-- Light falling straight down from above the top edge. -->
<fx:LightRays Angle="90" Spread="40" />

<!-- Light coming in from the top right. -->
<fx:LightRays Angle="135" />

Shape the fan

Spread is how wide the fan opens, in degrees, and Rays about how many rays cross it. Length is how far the light reaches, as a share of the shape's diagonal. Color tints the light and Intensity makes it stronger, up to 3.

<fx:LightRays Color="#BAE6FD" Spread="90" Rays="16" Length="1.2" Intensity="1.5" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Color Color #FFF1D6 The light's color.
Angle double 60 The way the light travels, degrees: 0 to the right, 90 down; positive turns clockwise. Range -180 to 180.
Spread double 60 How wide the fan of rays opens, degrees. Range 10–180.
Rays double 9 About how many rays across the fan. Range 2–40.
Length double 0.75 How far the light reaches, as a share of the shape's diagonal. Range 0.2–3.
Distance double 0.1 How far outside the shape the source sits, as a share of the diagonal. Range 0–1 (0–100%).

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On light rays, Intensity is the strength of the light, up to 3.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the rays add light. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze, kept fainter because haze reads heavier. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The rays hold still. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. About 0.08 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. The rays shimmer all the time, so the layer redraws every frame while it is active. See Performance.
  • Inside the shape. The light draws only within the shape, even though its source sits outside it, so it needs no room around the host.
