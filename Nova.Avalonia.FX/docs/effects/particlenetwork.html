Particle network

ParticleNetwork draws dots drifting across the shape, joined by fine lines whenever two come close: the closer they are, the stronger the line. The pointer joins in as one more point and draws the nearby dots toward it. Use it as a light, technical background for a landing page, a sign-in screen or a "connected" state. It's drawn with Skia shapes rather than a shader, clipped to the host's shape, and it has no background of its own.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a particle network

The network draws under the child, so leave the panel without a background and put the color on a container around the host:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <Border Background="#0B1220" CornerRadius="14">
        <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14">
            <fx:FXHost.Layers>
                <fx:ParticleNetwork Color="#CBD5E1" LineColor="#94A3B8" />
            </fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <Grid Height="240">
                <TextBlock Text="Connected" Foreground="White" FontSize="20" FontWeight="SemiBold"
                           HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
            </Grid>
        </fx:FXHost>
    </Border>
</UserControl>

Or draw it over a card that has its own background, as the gallery does, with Placement="Over":

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:ParticleNetwork Count="46" LinkDistance="70" Color="#CBD5E1" LineColor="#94A3B8" Placement="Over" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="260" Height="150" CornerRadius="14" Background="#0B1220" />
</fx:FXHost>

Fit it to the shape

Left unset, Count fits the shape: 60 dots, or fewer on a small shape, where the dots keep about 24 DIPs apart on average. LinkDistance, also left unset, is 100 DIPs, or less where the dots sit closer (at most 1.6 times their average spacing), so a small shape doesn't fill with lines. Set either one and it's drawn as set, however small the shape. The dots wander over the whole shape, whatever its size.

DotSize is the dots' size in DIPs, and Drift about how fast they drift, in DIPs a second; at 0 they hold still.

<fx:ParticleNetwork Count="120" LinkDistance="80" DotSize="1.5" Drift="20" />

Speed multiplies the clock and Drift sets the dots' own pace; changing either never makes the animation jump. Unlike the fills, the network's clock starts when it first draws, so networks on different hosts don't move in step.

Follow the pointer

With FollowPointer on (the default), the pointer over the shape joins the network: it links to the dots within reach and draws them a little toward it, and it eases after the pointer as it moves. Set FollowPointer="False" for a network that ignores the pointer.

PointAt joins the network from code, as if the pointer were there; null leaves:

Network.PointAt(new Point(120, 80));
Network.PointAt(null);

Methods

Call PointAt to aim the network from code: for keyboard focus, a guided tour or a demo.

Method Description
PointAt(Point? at) Joins the network at at (host coordinates) as the pointer would; null leaves.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Count int 60 How many dots. Left unset, it fits the shape: 60, or fewer on a small shape, where the dots keep about 24 DIPs apart on average. Range 4–200.
Color Color #94A3B8 The dots' color.
LineColor Color #94A3B8 The lines' color; each line fades as its two dots move apart.
LinkDistance double 100 How close two dots must be for a line between them. Left unset, it fits the shape: 100 DIPs, or less where the dots sit closer (at most 1.6 times their average spacing), so a small shape doesn't fill with lines. Range 20–300.
DotSize double 2 Diameter of a dot, in DIPs. Range 0.5–6.
Drift double 14 About how fast the dots drift, DIPs a second. Range 0–120 DIPs per second.
FollowPointer bool true Whether the pointer joins the network over the shape.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. Seed picks the dots' paths. RenderScale doesn't apply, since the network is drawn as shapes.

Notes

  • Not a fill: the network has no BorderWidth or UseMask, and it runs on its own clock.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the dots and lines are drawn in Color and LineColor on any surface. The default slate (#94A3B8) suits dark themes; the gallery uses a deeper slate (#64748B) on light ones. Pick a color for each theme with theme resources. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion: the dots hold still. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.09–0.32 ms of CPU time a frame on the benchmark's 340×190 card, and almost no GPU time. Close pairs are found through a grid rather than by testing every pair. See Performance.
