Fire

Fire sets the top edge of the host's shape alight: turbulent tongues that curl, narrow and tear, a white-yellow base fading to red tips, and embers flung up to drift and cool. Use it for a trending item, a streak, a hot deal or a game-like moment. Heat is a low, warm version for buttons, Plasma a thin band of energy around the border, and Simulated and Smoke run a small fluid simulation for fire and smoke that rise, curl and drift with the wind. The flames draw under the child and rise above it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add flames

The default style, Top, sends flames up from the top edge. Height sets how tall they are and Embers how many embers fly. Leave room above the host for the flames.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost Margin="0,90,0,0">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Fire Height="44" Embers="10" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="140" Height="58" CornerRadius="12" Background="#1C1917" Padding="12,0">
            <TextBlock Text="Trending" Foreground="White" FontWeight="SemiBold" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

LowColor, MidColor and HotColor are the temperature ramp, from the tips to the base. Scale sets the size of the turbulence, FlowSpeed how fast it rises and Warp how much it curls.

Styles

Each style sets its own colors, height, turbulence, embers and intensity at template priority. Set any of them yourself to override it.

Value Description
Top (default) Flames rising off the top edge, with embers.
Heat Low, warm tongues for UI: a hot button or a streak.
Plasma Contained energy hugging the border: a thin, breathing band of violet and ice-blue filaments flowing around it.
Embers Embers alone, rising off the top edge.
Simulated A small fluid simulation over the top edge: flames that rise, curl and tear into smoke. It carries momentum, so gusts and changes play out over time.
Smoke Simulated gray smoke that rises, curls and spreads, without flames.

Warm a button

Heat is low and warm, with few embers: enough to mark a button without covering what is around it.

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="999">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Fire Style="Heat" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="104" Height="36" CornerRadius="18" Background="#FF5A14">
        <TextBlock Text="Hot deal" Foreground="White" FontWeight="SemiBold"
                   HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
    </Border>
</fx:FXHost>

Simulated fire and smoke

Simulated steps a small fluid grid above the top edge, so the flames carry momentum: a gust or a change plays out over time. Height is the height of the space the fire and smoke fill, Wind pushes them sideways (positive to the right), Buoyancy sets how strongly heat rises and Swirl how much the flow curls. Intensity is how much heat is fed in. Smoke is the same simulation with gray smoke and no flames.

<fx:FXHost Margin="0,130,0,0">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Fire Style="Simulated" Height="120" Wind="0.4" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="200" Height="40" CornerRadius="8" Background="#1C1917" />
</fx:FXHost>

CellSize is the simulation's cell: smaller is finer and costs more.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Style FireStyle Top What burns: flames off the top edge, low heat for UI, plasma around the border, embers alone, or simulated fire or smoke. Choosing a style resets the other settings to its defaults (local values win). Values: Top, Heat, Plasma, Embers, Simulated, Smoke.
LowColor Color #8A1405 The ramp's low end: the cool tips of the flames (deep red by default); in plasma, the glow around the band (violet by default). Applies to the Top, Heat, Plasma, and Simulated styles.
MidColor Color #FF5A14 The ramp's middle: the body of the flames, or of the plasma band. Applies to the Top, Heat, Plasma, and Simulated styles.
HotColor Color #FFC65A The ramp's hot end: the bright base of the flames, or the plasma's filaments. Applies to the Top, Heat, Plasma, and Simulated styles.
Height double 54 Flame height; for plasma, sets the band's thickness with Sides; simulated, the height of the space above the shape the fire and smoke fill. Range 4–400 DIPs.
Scale double 20 Size of the turbulence. Range 4–80 DIPs. Applies to the Top, Heat, and Plasma styles.
FlowSpeed double 1.5 How fast the turbulence rises, in scales per second. Range 0–5. Applies to the Top, Heat, and Plasma styles.
Embers int 16 How many embers are in the air at once (0: none). Range 0–40. Applies to the Top, Heat, and Embers styles.
EmberRise double 58 How fast embers are flung up, DIPs a second; drag slows them to about a third of it. Range 0–200 DIPs per second. Applies to the Top, Heat, and Embers styles.
EmberSize double 0.8 Size of an ember, in DIPs. Range 0.2–3. Applies to the Top, Heat, and Embers styles.
EmberLife double 1.5 How long an ember glows, in seconds, on average; each lives 0.6 to 1.4 times this. Range 0.2–4. Applies to the Top, Heat, and Embers styles.
Warp double 1 How much the turbulence curls the tongues. Range 0–3. Applies to the Top and Heat styles.
Sides double 0.32 Plasma: the band's thickness, as a share of the height. Range 0–1 (0–100%). Applies to the Plasma style.
BaseGlow double 0.5 The fire's light on the edge it sits on. Range 0–2. Applies to the Top and Heat styles.
Occlusion double 0.25 How much the flames cover what is behind them. Range 0–1 (0–100%). Applies to the Top, Heat, and Plasma styles.
Swirl double 0 A slow sideways swirl in the flames; faster flow in plasma; simulated, how much the flow curls. Range 0–3. Applies to the Top, Heat, Plasma, Simulated, and Smoke styles.
SmokeColor Color #4A4A52 The smoke's color. Applies to the Simulated and Smoke styles.
CellSize double 4 Simulation cell size: smaller is finer and costs more. Range 2–12 DIPs. Applies to the Simulated and Smoke styles.
SmokeAmount double 0.6 How much smoke the fire gives off. Range 0–2. Applies to the Simulated and Smoke styles.
SmokeOpacity double 0.55 How dense the smoke looks. Range 0–1 (0–100%). Applies to the Simulated and Smoke styles.
Buoyancy double 1 How strongly heat rises. Range 0–3. Applies to the Simulated and Smoke styles.
Wind double 0 Sideways wind (positive: to the right). Range -2 to 2. Applies to the Simulated and Smoke styles.
Cooling double 1 How fast heat cools into smoke. Range 0.2–3. Applies to the Simulated style.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On fire, Intensity is the strength of the flames, up to 3.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. Fire reads best on dark UI. On a dark surface the flames add light. On a light surface the simulated flames draw as color over the smoke, and the procedural smoke veil and dim tips stay fainter. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The flames hold one pose and the embers stop; the simulated styles hold their current state. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. Procedural flames take about 0.23 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card, among the heavier layers; they only shade the area above the shape. The simulated styles take about 0.07 ms of GPU time plus the simulation step on the render thread, about 0.4 ms. In the browser the simulated fire takes 0.4–0.45 ms a frame plus 1.4–1.5 ms for its steps when interpreted, and 0.18–0.4 plus 0.35–0.8 ms when compiled ahead of time. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. The flames and embers rise above the top edge, and the simulated box reaches Height above it and a little past the sides. A clipping ancestor, such as a ScrollViewer, cuts them off: leave room above the host.
