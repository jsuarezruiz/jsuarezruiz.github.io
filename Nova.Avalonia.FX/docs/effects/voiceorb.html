Voice orb

Voice orb draws a living sphere of color: three soft lights drift inside a glassy orb, their colors meeting smoothly, with a glow around it. Bind Level to the microphone and the orb swells, its edge rolls and the lights drift faster as the voice rises. Use it as the face of a voice feature, a dictation button or a call screen. The orb is centered on the host's shape and draws under the child, so an icon or initials can sit on top.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a voice orb

Give the host a square child; the orb's radius is a share of its smaller half-size (Size). Here a microphone icon sits on the orb.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="56" Margin="40">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:VoiceOrb Level="{Binding MicLevel}" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="112" Height="112">
            <Path Data="M12,3 A3,3 0 0 0 9,6 L9,12 A3,3 0 0 0 15,12 L15,6 A3,3 0 0 0 12,3 Z M18,12 A6,6 0 0 1 6,12 M12,18 L12,21"
                  Stroke="White" StrokeThickness="2" StrokeLineCap="Round"
                  Width="26" Height="26" Stretch="Uniform" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Follow the voice

Level runs from 0 (quiet) to 1 (loud). Set it from your microphone meter as often as you get readings; the layer eases changes, rising quickly and falling more slowly, so a jumpy reading looks like a smooth swell.

// Orb is the VoiceOrb layer; called whenever the audio meter reports a new peak (0..1).
private void OnMeterLevel(double peak)
{
    Dispatcher.UIThread.Post(() => Orb.Level = peak);
}

Color and shape

Color, SecondColor and ThirdColor are the three lights; they blend through their own middle rather than through gray. Size sets the orb at rest, Glow the light around it (0 for none), and Turbulence how much the lights bend as they drift.

<fx:VoiceOrb Color="#34D399" SecondColor="#22D3EE" ThirdColor="#A78BFA"
             Size="0.7" Glow="1.4" Turbulence="0.6" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Level double 0 How loud, 0..1: bind it to the microphone level. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Color Color #7C5CFF The first light's color.
SecondColor Color #22D3EE The second light's color.
ThirdColor Color #EC4899 The third light's color.
Size double 0.8 The orb's radius at rest, as a share of the shape's smaller half-size. Range 0.3–1 (30–100%).
Glow double 1 The glow around the orb, a multiple: 1 is the default, 0 none. Range 0–2.
Turbulence double 1 How much the lights bend as they drift. Range 0–2.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On the orb, Intensity dims the layer, and Seed sets where each light starts its orbit, so two orbs never move in step.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. The orb looks much the same on both; its glassy rim is fainter on a light surface. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The colors hold still, and the orb still swells with Level. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. The orb moves all the time, even at a Level of 0, so it redraws every frame while it is active. Its glow covers more area than most layers (about 0.8 Mpx on the bench's 340×190 card), and the cost grows with the area. Set IsActive to False while the app isn't listening. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. The glow reaches past the shape by about the orb's radius. A clipping ancestor cuts it off: leave room around the host.
