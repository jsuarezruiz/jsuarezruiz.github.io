Voice wave

Voice wave draws glowing waves across the host's shape in three colors, crossing each other under an envelope that pinches them to nothing at both ends. Quiet, they lie almost flat and drift slowly; bind Level to the microphone and they swell and drift faster as the voice rises. Use it under a dictation field, in a call bar or beside a voice orb. The waves fill the shape from side to side and draw under the child.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a voice wave

The waves span the child's width and rise into its height, so give the child the size the waves should take. An empty Border is enough.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <StackPanel Spacing="6">
        <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="0">
            <fx:FXHost.Layers>
                <fx:VoiceWave Level="{Binding MicLevel}" />
            </fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <Border Width="250" Height="96" />
        </fx:FXHost>
        <TextBlock Text="Listening…" HorizontalAlignment="Center" Opacity="0.6" />
    </StackPanel>
</UserControl>

Follow the voice

Level runs from 0 (quiet) to 1 (loud). Set it from the microphone meter as often as readings arrive; the layer eases changes, rising quickly and falling more slowly, and speeds up the drift without the waves jumping.

// Wave is the VoiceWave layer; called whenever the audio meter reports a new peak (0..1).
private void OnMeterLevel(double peak)
{
    Dispatcher.UIThread.Post(() => Wave.Level = peak);
}

To pair it with a voice orb, bind both to the same level.

Color and size

Color, SecondColor and ThirdColor are the three waves. Amplitude is how high they rise at full level, as a share of the half height. LineWidth sets the thickness of each line and Glow the light around it (0 for none).

<fx:VoiceWave Color="#F472B6" SecondColor="#FBBF24" ThirdColor="#34D399"
              Amplitude="0.6" LineWidth="1.5" Glow="1.3" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Level double 0 How loud, 0..1: bind it to the microphone level. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Color Color #7C5CFF The first wave's color.
SecondColor Color #22D3EE The second wave's color.
ThirdColor Color #EC4899 The third wave's color.
Amplitude double 0.8 How high the waves rise at full level, as a share of the half height. Range 0.1–1 (10–100%).
LineWidth double 2 Width of each wave's line, in DIPs. Range 0.5–8.
Glow double 1 The glow around the waves, a multiple: 1 is the default, 0 none. Range 0–2.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a voice wave, Intensity dims the layer and Seed doesn't apply.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the waves add light. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze in their own colors. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The waves hold still, and they still swell with Level. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. About 0.05 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. The waves drift even at a Level of 0, so the layer redraws every frame while it is active: set IsActive to False while the app isn't listening. See Performance.
  • Inside the shape. The waves draw only within the shape, so they need no room around the host; Amplitude is a share of the shape's half height.
