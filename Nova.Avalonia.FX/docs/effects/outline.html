Outline

Outline strokes the child's own shape at an exact width: the letters of a label, an icon, a cut-out picture or any control. Use it for outlined display type, a ring around a selected icon, or a die-cut sticker with a soft shadow. It follows the child's pixels, not its box: when the host captures the child, it measures how far each pixel is from the edge, so the stroke keeps its width around any shape.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add an outline

Wrap the content in a host. Bare text shows the effect best:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Outline Color="#7C3AED" Width="3" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <TextBlock Text="Nova FX" FontSize="40" FontWeight="Black" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Styles

Value Description
Stroke (default) A band of an exact width around the content.
Sticker A thick border with a soft shadow under it, as if cut out and stuck on the page.

Make a sticker

Style="Sticker" draws a thick border with a soft shadow under it, as if the content were cut out and stuck on the page. Switching to it sets Width to 6 at template priority, so a local Width still wins. ShadowColor, ShadowOffset (how far the shadow falls below) and ShadowBlur shape the shadow:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Outline Style="Sticker" ShadowOffset="4" ShadowBlur="8" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="hello!" FontSize="44" FontWeight="Black" Foreground="#EC4899" />
</fx:FXHost>

Stroke outside or over the edge

A stroke sits outside the content by default (Position="Outside"), so the content keeps its own size, and the layer draws under the child. Position="Center" puts the stroke half in and half out of the edge, and the layer draws over the child. Softness fades the stroke's edge:

<fx:Outline Position="Center" Color="#22D3EE" Width="2" Softness="0.5" />

Position applies to the Stroke style only.

Keep it in step with the content

The host captures the child again when one of the child's own properties changes, but it doesn't watch the child's descendants. After changing something deeper inside it, call RefreshMask:

// BadgeText is a TextBlock inside the StackPanel that is the host's child.
BadgeText.Text = "PRO";
BadgeHost.RefreshMask();

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Style OutlineStyle Stroke A plain stroke, or a sticker: a thick border with a soft shadow. Choosing a style sets Width to 3 or 6 DIPs (a local value wins). Values: Stroke, Sticker.
Color Color #FFFFFF The stroke's color; its alpha is the stroke's opacity.
Width double 3 Width of the stroke, in DIPs. Range 0.5–24.
Softness double 0 How far the stroke's edge fades, DIPs (0: crisp). Range 0–8.
Position OutlinePosition Outside Whether the stroke sits outside the content or centered on its edge. Applies to the Stroke style. Values: Outside, Center.
ShadowColor Color #5A000000 The color of the sticker's shadow; its alpha is how dark the shadow is. Applies to the Sticker style.
ShadowOffset double 3 How far the sticker's shadow falls below it. Range 0–12 DIPs. Applies to the Sticker style.
ShadowBlur double 6 How soft the sticker's shadow is, in DIPs. Range 0–16. Applies to the Sticker style.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. The outline is still, so Speed and Seed don't apply.

Notes

  • It follows the content's pixels. See Content masks. A pixel counts as inside when its alpha is at least half the child's own strongest alpha, so text in a translucent color still has a shape.
  • Width is bounded. The distance field reaches 47.75 DIPs outside the edge; Width is kept to 24 DIPs so the stroke and a sticker's shadow stay within it.
  • It draws past the child's box by the stroke's width, plus the shadow for a sticker. A clipping ancestor, such as a ScrollViewer or a Border with ClipToBounds, cuts it: leave room around the host.
  • Light and dark surfaces: it draws the same on both. A white outline vanishes on a white page or around white text; the gallery uses #7C3AED on light and #A78BFA on dark, from a theme resource (see Light and dark surfaces).
  • Reduced motion: nothing moves, so nothing changes.
  • Cost: drawing is cheap (about 0.01 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark card, 0.03 ms for a sticker) and happens once, since the outline doesn't animate. The cost is in the capture: building the distance field takes a few milliseconds on the UI thread each time the child changes, never per frame. See Content masks and Performance.
