Rain glass

RainGlass puts rain on a misted pane over the content: beads that come and go, fine droplets, and drops that slide down in fits and starts, leaving a clear wet trail dotted with droplets. Each drop is a small lens that shows what is behind it upside down, and Fog mists the glass between them. Use it for a weather card, a cozy header or a lock screen. It draws over the child, so it rains on the child and whatever shows through it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add rain

Wrap the content in a host. Points of light make the lensing easy to see:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:RainGlass />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="260" Height="150" CornerRadius="14" Background="#101830" ClipToBounds="True">
            <Canvas>
                <Ellipse Canvas.Left="20" Canvas.Top="30" Width="26" Height="26" Fill="#FFB347" />
                <Ellipse Canvas.Left="120" Canvas.Top="24" Width="30" Height="30" Fill="#7FD4FF" />
                <Ellipse Canvas.Left="170" Canvas.Top="80" Width="22" Height="22" Fill="#FFD27F" />
                <Ellipse Canvas.Left="40" Canvas.Top="100" Width="20" Height="20" Fill="#FFD43B" />
            </Canvas>
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Give it something to bend

The pane copies what is under it just before it draws (see Layers that read the backdrop). Over the child, the default, that is the child and whatever shows through it. Each drop shows what lies about two of its radii away, mirrored, so the drops read best over detail: lights, a picture, text. Inside an offscreen layer (an ancestor with Opacity below 1, an effect, or a clip that needs a layer) the copy can't see that ancestor's content, so keep such ancestors opaque.

Clear or mist the glass

Fog is how misted the glass is between the drops, from 0 (clear) to 1. The mist blurs what is under it, labels included: the gallery lowers it to 0.3 over a picture so its titles stay readable:

<fx:RainGlass Fog="0.3" />

Change the rain

Density sets how many drops there are, DropSize the radius of a typical drop, and Refraction how strongly the drops bend what is behind them (0 for none):

<fx:RainGlass Density="1.6" DropSize="9" Refraction="1.4" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Density double 1 How many drops. Range 0–2.
DropSize double 6 Radius of a typical drop. Range 2–16 DIPs.
Fog double 0.6 How misted the glass is between the drops. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Refraction double 1 How strongly the drops bend what is behind them, a multiple: 1 shows what lies about two radii away, 0 none. Range 0–2.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. Seed changes where the drops fall, and Speed how fast the rain runs.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces: it draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the drops hold still.
  • Cost: about 0.51 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark card, including a blurred copy of what is under it. It animates, so it redraws every frame while it is visible. See Performance.
