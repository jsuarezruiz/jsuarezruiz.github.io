Page peel

Page peel curls a corner of the element up when it is hovered, like a sticker or a page, showing its back with a shadow under the curl. It peels further as the pointer nears the corner and settles back when the pointer leaves. Use it on stickers, notes and cards, or as a hint that there is more underneath. While peeled, the layer draws a capture of the content over it.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a page peel

Add the layer to a host around the element; hover it to peel:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="16" HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:PagePeel Amount="0.22" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="200" Height="120" CornerRadius="16" Background="#f97316">
            <TextBlock Text="Sticker" Foreground="White" FontWeight="Bold" FontSize="18"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Hold it peeled

IsPeeled holds the corner peeled without a hover, for example while a card has notes on its back:

<fx:PagePeel x:Name="CardPeel" IsPeeled="{Binding HasNotes}" />

private void OnTogglePeel(object? sender, RoutedEventArgs e) => CardPeel.IsPeeled = !CardPeel.IsPeeled;

A peel set in XAML before the content is laid out waits for the first layout pass, then peels. Hovering still peels while IsPeeled is false; the corner settles back when neither holds it.

Pick the corner and the curl

Corner picks the corner that curls (BottomRight by default). Amount is how far it peels on hover, as a share of the shape's diagonal, and CurlRadius the largest radius of the curl. BackColor colors the back, and Shadow sets the strength of the shadow the curl casts.

<fx:PagePeel Corner="TopRight" Amount="0.3" CurlRadius="14" BackColor="#f4f1ea" Shadow="0.5" />

On a round shape, such as an avatar, the corner lies mostly outside the circle: raise Amount so the peel reaches the edge. BackColor is a plain color; for a back that follows the theme, bind it to a DynamicResource color.

Follow the pointer, or not

With FollowPointer on (the default), the corner peels further as the pointer nears it. Turn it off for a fixed peel of Amount while hovered. Duration is how long the corner takes to peel or settle back (0.28 seconds by default); the layer's Speed doesn't apply.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Corner FXCorner BottomRight The corner that curls up (default: the bottom right). Values: TopLeft, TopRight, BottomRight, BottomLeft.
Amount double 0.16 How far it peels on hover, as a share of the shape's diagonal. Range 0.02–0.6 (2–60%).
CurlRadius double 10 Radius of the curl, at most: a short peel rolls tighter, so a flap always shows. Range 2–60 DIPs.
BackColor Color #F4F1EA The color of the back.
Shadow double 0.35 How dark the shadow under the curl is (0: none). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Duration double 0.28 How long it takes to peel or settle back. Range 0.05–1 seconds.
FollowPointer bool true Peels further as the pointer nears the corner.
IsPeeled bool false Holds it peeled without a hover.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. Page peel has no randomness and no clock of its own, so Seed and Speed do nothing. While IsActive is off, it doesn't peel.

Notes

  • A capture while peeled. The capture is taken when the peel begins, and the real content is hidden under it but still takes input. Changes to the content show once the corner has settled back.
  • Light and dark. The page draws the same on both surfaces; pick a BackColor that suits your theme.
  • Reduced motion. A quick peel. See the poses.
  • Cost. Under 0.1 ms of GPU time a frame on a card while it peels; flat and not hovered, it draws nothing. See What a layer costs.
