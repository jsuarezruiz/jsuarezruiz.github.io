Liquid metal

A slab of polished metal that reflects a photo studio: a bright sky above a soft horizon, strip lights and a dark floor, seen in a domed face with a rolled rim. The surface ripples and flows slowly. Use it for a premium button, a badge, an avatar, a metal border around a card, or a chrome logo. It is a fill: it paints the host's shape and goes under the child, or paints the child's own pixels with UseMask.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add liquid metal

The fill goes under the child, so leave the child without a background:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:LiquidMetal Preset="Gold" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="200" Height="48" CornerRadius="24" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

Each preset sets Tint, LightColor, Iridescence and Contrast. Set any of them yourself to adjust a preset; your value wins.

Value Description
Chrome (default) Bright, cool silver with a faint rainbow sheen.
Gold Warm yellow gold under warm light, with no rainbow sheen.
Rose Pink rose gold under soft, warm light.
Gunmetal Dark blue-gray steel with deeper shadows, sharper highlights and a hint of rainbow sheen.

Make a chrome border

BorderWidth paints only a band inside the edge, shaded as a round tube, and leaves the middle clear for the content:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:LiquidMetal Preset="Chrome" BorderWidth="5" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="200" Height="48" CornerRadius="12">
        <TextBlock Text="Chrome tube border" HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
    </Border>
</fx:FXHost>

Turn text or a logo into metal

With UseMask, the metal paints the child's own pixels instead of its box, and goes over the child:

<fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:LiquidMetal UseMask="True" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <TextBlock Text="CHROME" FontSize="30" FontWeight="Black" Foreground="White" />
</fx:FXHost>

The host captures the child to find its pixels. After changing something deeper inside the child, call FXHost.RefreshMask(); see Content masks.

Pin the reflection to the window

By default the reflection moves with the element, so every shape of the same size shows the same picture. With Anchor="Window", it is pinned to the window like a fixed studio light: neighboring shapes show one continuous surface, and the reflection slides over them as they scroll.

<StackPanel Orientation="Horizontal" Spacing="8">
    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers><fx:LiquidMetal Anchor="Window" /></fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="56" Height="56" CornerRadius="12" />
    </fx:FXHost>
    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers><fx:LiquidMetal Anchor="Window" /></fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="56" Height="56" CornerRadius="12" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</StackPanel>

Shape the metal

Tint is the metal's own color and LightColor the studio's. Flow sets how far the surface ripples, Scale the ripple size and FlowSpeed how fast they move. Bevel is the width of the rolled rim, Dome how curved the face is, and LightAngle turns the studio around. A still copper slab:

<fx:LiquidMetal Tint="#D9915F" LightColor="#FFE8D6" Flow="0" Dome="0.3" />

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset LiquidMetalPreset Chrome The metal: sets Tint, LightColor, Iridescence and Contrast. Local values win. Values: Chrome, Gold, Rose, Gunmetal.
Tint Color #E9ECF0 Reflectance at normal incidence: near white for chrome, warm for gold.
LightColor Color #FFFFFF The studio lights' color.
Flow double 0.3 How far the surface ripples (0: a still, polished slab). Range 0–2.
Scale double 150 Ripple size. Range 10–400 DIPs.
FlowSpeed double 0.35 How fast the ripples flow (0: frozen). Range 0–2 per second.
Bevel double 14 Width of the rolled rim. Range 0–48 DIPs.
Dome double 0.55 How domed the face is: 0 flat, 1 strongly curved; negative dishes it inward, like a keycap. Range -1 to 1.5.
LightAngle double -7 Tilt of the studio around the view axis, degrees: 0 upright, its lights above; positive turns it counterclockwise. Range -180 to 180.
Iridescence double 0.08 Rainbow thin-film color where the surface bends hardest (0: none). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Contrast double 1 1: studio as lit; higher deepens the floor and sharpens the lights. Range 0.5–2.5.
Anchor FXAnchor Element Element (the default): the reflection moves with the element. Window: it is pinned to the window, so neighboring shapes show one continuous surface and it slides over them as they scroll, like a fixed studio light. Values: Element, Window.
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Intensity is the fill's coverage, from 0 to 1, so a transition on it fades the metal in and out.
  • Text on the metal: the upper part reflects the studio's bright sky, and the middle sits on the horizon's dark reflections. With a light tint (chrome, gold, rose), dark text reads near the top, such as a card's title; centered labels read best in white.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the metal draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the surface holds one pose. With Flow or FlowSpeed at 0 it is still anyway, and requests no frames.
  • Cost: 0.312 ms of GPU time per frame on a 340×190 card in the gallery's bench, among the heavier fills. On a 700×500 panel it takes 0.73 ms, and 0.47 ms with RenderScale="0.5". See Render scale.
  • Fills share one clock, so equal fills on different hosts move in step.
