Fog

Fog draws banks of mist drifting sideways across the shape, thickest at the bottom and thinning out toward the top of the bank. Use it for mood: a game's title screen, a weather card, a night scene. By default it goes over the child and hangs in front of the content; under a see-through panel, it's a background.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add fog

Put the host around a card. The mist drifts over it, clipped to its rounded shape:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost>
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Fog Height="0.7" Scale="90" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="236" Height="128" CornerRadius="14" Padding="16">
            <Border.Background>
                <LinearGradientBrush StartPoint="0%,0%" EndPoint="100%,100%">
                    <GradientStop Color="#1F2937" Offset="0" />
                    <GradientStop Color="#334155" Offset="0.6" />
                    <GradientStop Color="#0F172A" Offset="1" />
                </LinearGradientBrush>
            </Border.Background>
            <TextBlock Text="Morning mist" Foreground="White" FontWeight="Bold" FontSize="18" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Shape the bank

Height is how far up the shape the bank reaches, as a share of its height (0.6 by default; 1 covers it all). Density is how thick the mist is, Scale the size of the wisps in DIPs, and Color the mist's color.

<fx:Fog Height="1" Density="0.5" Scale="200" Color="#CBD5E1" />

The bank adapts to the shape, so the same settings work on a button and on a page. A short shape (under about 200 DIPs tall) gets a bank up to 1.6 times higher and a denser mist, so it still shows fog. And past 0.9 of the shape's height (0.6 on a short shape, and at least 24 DIPs), Scale counts only a quarter, so a small shape still shows separate banks.

Set the drift

DriftSpeed is how fast the mist drifts sideways, in DIPs a second: positive drifts right, negative left (18 by default). At 0 the banks stay in place but still churn slowly.

<fx:Fog DriftSpeed="-40" />

Speed multiplies the whole clock, the drift and the churn, and DriftSpeed sets the drift alone; changing either never makes the animation jump. Unlike the fills, fog's clock starts when it first draws, so fog on different hosts doesn't move in step.

Put the fog behind the content

With Placement="Under", the mist sits behind the child instead. Leave the child without a background so the mist shows through it, and put the color on a container around the host:

<Border Background="#0F172A" CornerRadius="14">
    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Fog Placement="Under" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Grid Height="220">
            <TextBlock Text="Night trail" Foreground="White" FontSize="24" FontWeight="Bold"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Bottom" Margin="0,0,0,24" />
        </Grid>
    </fx:FXHost>
</Border>

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Color Color #E8EDF2 The mist's color.
Density double 0.75 How thick the mist is; at 1 its densest wisps are opaque. Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Height double 0.6 How far up the shape the bank reaches (1: all of it). A short shape (under about 200 DIPs) gets up to 1.6 times this, and a denser mist, so a button still shows fog. Range 0.05–1 (5–100%).
Scale double 120 The size of the wisps. Past 0.9 of the shape's height (0.6 on a shape under about 200 DIPs; at least 24 DIPs) it counts only a quarter, so a small shape still shows banks. Range 20–600.
DriftSpeed double 18 How fast it drifts sideways, DIPs a second; positive drifts right, negative left. Range -120 to 120 DIPs per second.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. Seed picks the shape of the banks.

Notes

  • Not a fill: fog has no BorderWidth or UseMask, and it runs on its own clock.
  • Light and dark surfaces: the mist is drawn in Color on any surface. The default, a pale gray, shows best over dark or moody content and barely shows over white; over light content, pick a deeper color. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion: the mist holds still. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: about 0.25 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark's 340×190 card. The cost grows with the area; the mist is soft, so on a large panel RenderScale="0.5" suits it. See Performance.
