Heat haze

HeatHaze makes what is behind it shimmer like hot air, wobbling in rising, turbulent noise. By default a column of hot air rises above the shape, as wide as it and strongest a little way up, so it pairs naturally with Fire. With WholeShape it fills the shape instead. Use it above flames, for an overheating warning, or on a summer or desert header. It bends what is already drawn there: the area above the host, or the host's own content.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add heat haze

Pair it with a fire: the flames draw under the child, so the haze, which draws over it, bends them as they rise:

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="12" Margin="0,100,0,0">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Fire />
            <fx:HeatHaze Height="90" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="200" Height="60" CornerRadius="12" Background="#1C1210" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Give it something to bend

The haze copies the region it covers just before it draws (see Layers that read the backdrop), so what it bends depends on what is drawn there first:

  • Something must be above the host: flames from a layer under the child, text, a picture or the page's own pattern. Over a flat color there is nothing to bend.
  • The content goes first. Controls drawn after the host aren't bent: they sit in front of the haze.
  • Leave room above. The column is drawn past the host's top edge, up to Height. A clipping ancestor, such as a ScrollViewer or a Border with ClipToBounds, cuts it.
  • Keep ancestors opaque. Inside an offscreen layer (an ancestor with Opacity below 1, an effect, or a clip that needs a layer) the copy can't see that ancestor's content.

Here, as in the gallery, heat rises off a button and bends the line of type above it:

<StackPanel Spacing="8">
    <TextBlock Text="Hot air above" FontSize="28" FontWeight="Bold" HorizontalAlignment="Center" />
    <fx:FXHost HorizontalAlignment="Center" CornerRadius="12">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:HeatHaze Height="100" Displacement="3" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Button Content="Generate" Width="220" Height="56" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</StackPanel>

Fill the shape instead

WholeShape="True" puts the hot air over the shape itself, so it bends the child and whatever shows through it, and nothing above:

<fx:HeatHaze WholeShape="True" Displacement="2" Scale="12" />

Displacement is how far pixels move, Scale the size of the turbulence, and FlowSpeed how fast it rises.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Height double 40 Height of the column of hot air above the shape. Range 4–200 DIPs.
Scale double 9 Size of the turbulence. Range 2–60 DIPs.
FlowSpeed double 2 How fast the hot air rises, in turbulence sizes per second (0: still). Range 0–6.
Displacement double 1.1 How far pixels move. Range 0–8 DIPs.
WholeShape bool false Fill the shape instead of rising above it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces: it draws the same on both.
  • Reduced motion: the haze holds still: what is behind keeps a fixed ripple instead of wobbling.
  • Cost: about 0.15–0.25 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark card. It shades only the column above the shape, not around it. With FlowSpeed or Displacement at 0 it doesn't animate. See Performance.
