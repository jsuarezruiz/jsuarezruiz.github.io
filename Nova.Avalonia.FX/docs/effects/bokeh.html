Bokeh

Bokeh paints soft, out-of-focus lights drifting at three depths over a background, the way a lens renders lights it isn't focused on: round discs with brighter rims, the nearest ones biggest and fastest. Use it behind an evening event, a music or nightlife screen, or a festive banner. It's a fill: it paints the host's shape under the child.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add bokeh

Put the host around the panel, and leave the panel without a background so the lights show through it.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="16">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:Bokeh />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Grid Height="260">
            <TextBlock Text="Tonight" Foreground="White" FontSize="30" FontWeight="Bold"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
        </Grid>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Presets

A preset picks the palette of the lights:

Value Description
City (default) Warm street lights with a few cool ones.
Sunset Coral, orange, yellow and pink lights, with a touch of violet.
Neon Aqua, blue, purple, pink and yellow lights, like neon signs.

Colors takes up to six colors of your own, and each light takes one of them at random. BackgroundColor is the color behind the lights.

<fx:Bokeh Preset="Neon" />

<fx:Bokeh Colors="#FDE68A, #F472B6, #60A5FA" BackgroundColor="#111827" />

Set the focus and the drift

Size is the radius of the nearest lights in DIPs; farther ones are smaller. Focus sets how sharp the discs' edges are, from 0 (very soft) to 1. Density sets how many lights there are. Drift multiplies how fast they drift, and Twinkle how much they brighten and dim, from 0 (steady) to 1. With both at 0 the lights hold still, and the layer asks for no frames.

<fx:Bokeh Size="60" Focus="0.7" Density="0.6" Drift="0.4" Twinkle="0" />

To change the pace while it runs, change Speed or Drift: neither makes the animation jump, and the lights carry on from where they are. Fills run on one shared clock, so bokeh on different hosts, with the same settings from the start, drifts in step.

Float the lights over content

Set BackgroundColor to a transparent color to keep only the lights. Under the child, they then show wherever the child is see-through; with Placement="Over", they float over the content, such as a photo:

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="16">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:Bokeh BackgroundColor="Transparent" Placement="Over" Density="0.5" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Image Source="/Assets/city-night.jpg" Width="360" Height="240" Stretch="UniformToFill" />
</fx:FXHost>

The lights are added as light, so they show best over dark content.

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Preset BokehPreset City The palette of the lights, used while Colors is unset. Values: City, Sunset, Neon.
Colors FXColorList Unset The colors (up to six). Unset: the preset's palette.
BackgroundColor Color #0B0F1D The color behind the lights; transparent shows what is behind the host. To lay the lights over the content, also set Placement to Over.
Density double 1 How many lights. Range 0.2–3.
Size double 34 Radius of the nearest lights, DIPs; farther ones are smaller. Range 4–120.
Focus double 0.3 How sharp the discs' edges are (0: very soft). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
Drift double 1 How fast the lights drift down, a multiple; nearer lights move faster (0: still). Range 0–3.
Twinkle double 0.4 How much each light slowly dims and brightens (0: steady). Range 0–1 (0–100%).
BorderWidth double 0 Width of a band inside the edge, DIPs. 0: the whole shape. Range 0–48.
UseMask bool false Paints only the child's own pixels (text, icons, a logo) instead of the shape: gradient text, a liquid-metal logo, holographic icons. It then goes over the child unless Placement says otherwise. The host captures the child to know its pixels: call RefreshMask after changing something deep inside it.

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a fill, Intensity is its coverage, up to 1, and Seed picks where the lights are.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces: bokeh doesn't change with the surface. Its default background is dark, so it looks the same in both themes; with a transparent background, the lights fade out over a light surface. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion: the lights hold one fixed pose. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost: 0.44 ms of GPU time a frame on the benchmark's 340×190 card, among the heavier backgrounds. The cost grows with the area; the discs are soft, so on a large panel RenderScale="0.5" suits it. See Performance.
  • Bands and masks: like every fill, it has BorderWidth, which paints only a band inside the edge, and UseMask, which paints only the child's own pixels, such as large text. See Content masks.
