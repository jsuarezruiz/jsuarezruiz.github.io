Border beam

Border beam sends streaks of light running around the host's border, each fading from a bright head to its tail color, with a soft glow on either side. Use it on a chat input, a "new" badge, or a card that is working on something. The streaks run along the edge of the shape, following its corner radius, and draw over the child.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add a border beam

Color is the bright head and TailColor the end of the streak. Period is the time for one lap, in seconds, and Length the length of each streak along the border.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="22">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:BorderBeam Period="5" Length="110" />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <TextBox PlaceholderText="Ask anything…" Width="260" Height="44" CornerRadius="22"
                 Padding="18,0" VerticalContentAlignment="Center" BorderThickness="0" />
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Run several streaks

Count spreads up to four streaks evenly around the border. IsReversed runs them the other way around, and Offset sets where the first one starts, as a share of a lap.

<fx:BorderBeam Count="2" IsReversed="True" Color="#22D3EE" TailColor="#7C5CFF" Length="80" />

Width is the thickness of the stroke. Glow scales the soft light on either side of it (0 for none) and GlowRadius sets how far that light spreads.

Show it while working

Bind IsActive to show the beam only while something is in progress. Switched off, the layer draws nothing and costs nothing.

<fx:FXHost CornerRadius="16">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:BorderBeam IsActive="{Binding IsGenerating}" Period="3" />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Border Width="280" Height="140" CornerRadius="16" Background="#15171C" />
</fx:FXHost>

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Color Color #FFAA40 The color at a streak's bright head.
TailColor Color #9C40FF The color a streak fades to at its tail.
Length double 120 Length of each streak along the border. Range 8–1000 DIPs.
Period double 6 Seconds for one lap. Range 0.5–30.
Width double 1.5 Thickness of a streak's line, in DIPs. Range 0.5–8.
Glow double 0.6 The soft light either side of a streak, a multiple: 1 is full, 0 none. Range 0–2.
GlowRadius double 6 How far the glow spreads either side of a streak, in DIPs. Range 1–30.
Count int 1 Streaks, evenly spaced around the border. Range 1–4.
IsReversed bool false Runs the other way around.
Offset double 0 Where the first streak starts, as a share of a lap. Range 0–1 (0–100%).

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On a border beam, Intensity dims the layer and Seed doesn't apply.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the streaks add light. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze, and the glow stays mostly inside the shape, so it doesn't smudge the surface around it. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The streaks hold still. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. About 0.03 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. It shades only a ring around the border, not the interior. See Performance.
  • Room around the host. The glow spreads a little past the edge, about 2.5 × GlowRadius. A clipping ancestor cuts it off: leave a few DIPs of room around the host.
