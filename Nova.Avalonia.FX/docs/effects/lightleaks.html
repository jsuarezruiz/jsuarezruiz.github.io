Light leaks

Light leaks brings warm film light in over the edges of the host's shape: four long, soft glows that drift along the frame and breathe in and out, whitening where they are hottest, with a faint, fast flicker. Use it on a photo, a hero card or a memories screen to give it a nostalgic, analog feel. It draws over the child, inside the shape, as added light.

  • Light
  • Dark

Add light leaks

Put the layer over a picture or a dark, moody card. The leaks show best where there is something darker for them to light.

<UserControl xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
             xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
             xmlns:fx="https://github.com/jsuarezruiz/Nova.Avalonia.FX">

    <fx:FXHost CornerRadius="14">
        <fx:FXHost.Layers>
            <fx:LightLeaks />
        </fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <Border Width="260" Height="150" CornerRadius="14">
            <Border.Background>
                <LinearGradientBrush StartPoint="0%,0%" EndPoint="100%,100%">
                    <GradientStop Color="#2B1D16" Offset="0" />
                    <GradientStop Color="#51402F" Offset="0.6" />
                    <GradientStop Color="#1C2430" Offset="1" />
                </LinearGradientBrush>
            </Border.Background>
            <TextBlock Text="Summer '98" Foreground="#FFF3E0" FontWeight="Bold" FontSize="22"
                       HorizontalAlignment="Center" VerticalAlignment="Center" />
        </Border>
    </fx:FXHost>
</UserControl>

Color and size

Color, SecondColor and ThirdColor tint the leaks; the defaults are warm oranges and pinks. Size scales the leaks with the shape, and Flicker sets the fast, faint flicker of film running through a gate (0 for none). Intensity makes the light stronger, up to 3.

<fx:LightLeaks Color="#7C5CFF" SecondColor="#22D3EE" ThirdColor="#EC4899"
               Size="1.4" Flicker="0" Intensity="1.4" />

Light a dark picture in a light app

The host picks its surface from its theme. In a light theme, light layers draw as tinted haze, which is meant for light content. When the content under the leaks is dark, set the surface yourself, so they add light as they do on a dark theme:

<fx:FXHost Surface="Dark" CornerRadius="14">
    <fx:FXHost.Layers>
        <fx:LightLeaks />
    </fx:FXHost.Layers>
    <Image Source="/Assets/beach-at-dusk.jpg" Width="300" Height="200" />
</fx:FXHost>

Properties

Property Type Default Description
Color Color #FF6A2B One leak's color; a fourth leak takes a mix of this and SecondColor.
SecondColor Color #FFB547 Another leak's color.
ThirdColor Color #FF2D6F The third leak's color.
Size double 1 Size of the leaks, as a multiple of the shape's size. Range 0.2–2.
Flicker double 0.3 A fast, faint flicker, like film running through a gate. Range 0–1 (0–100%).

Every layer also has the common properties: Intensity, Opacity, Speed, Seed, IsActive, Placement, BlendMode and RenderScale. On light leaks, Intensity is the strength of the light, up to 3.

Notes

  • Light and dark surfaces. On a dark surface the leaks add light. On a light surface they draw as tinted haze. See Light and dark surfaces.
  • Reduced motion. The leaks hold still. See Reduced motion.
  • Cost. About 0.1–0.2 ms of GPU time a frame on the bench's 340×190 card. The leaks move all the time, so the layer redraws every frame while it is active. See Performance.
  • Inside the shape. The light draws only within the shape, so it needs no room around the host.
